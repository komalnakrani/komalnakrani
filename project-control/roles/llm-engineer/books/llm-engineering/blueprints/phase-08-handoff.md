## Mandatory chapter production checks

Every manuscript chapter must:

- realize its blueprint's objectives, sequence, skill procedure, Mosaic transition, failure injection, exercises, and pass evidence;
- use the exact three assigned chapter claims and resolve every `LLME-BSRC-*` record, including limitations;
- keep every `LLME-CASE-*` bounded to its published/official context and label Mosaic Desk as fictional/synthetic;
- distinguish source statement, author synthesis, constructed scenario, and normative recommendation;
- use provider-neutral contracts and isolate managed/open-weight differences in named adapters/responsibility callouts;
- preserve proposal-only Mosaic authority: no warranty approval, safety-critical decision, customer contact, part order, or system-of-record mutation;
- retain product, domain, legal/license, privacy, security/safety, platform/SRE/MLOps, AI Research, FDE, and agentic-system handoffs;
- explain durable principles in the main flow and date volatile provider/model/runtime/hardware examples;
- end with a usable outgoing dossier artifact, limitations, assessment, and bridge to the next chapter.

## Evidence and currentness gate

Before freezing each batch, reopen every URL used and record the check date. Reverify provider deprecations, model identifiers, structured-output behavior, caching/pricing, context limits, trainer/runtime APIs, hardware/kernel support, and serving guidance. Do not reproduce benchmark results as current rankings or portable performance promises. Automated graders, RAG metrics, model cards, schemas, safe serialization, hashes, and safety guidance keep their explicit “does not prove” limitations.

No real customer, production, ROI, safety, quality, or latency outcome may be invented. Exercise measurements must be clearly synthetic or produced by a future authorized reproducible artifact.

## Visual handoff during writing

Insert callouts only for the exact 66 accepted IDs. Manuscript production supplies figure anchor, caption, essential-label inventory, short alt, and long description. It creates no SVG. Later Phase 10 uses ImageGen-only colorful, crisp, artistic 3D/realistic raster. Labels must match the blueprint and pass spelling/legibility review. Komal appears only when pedagogically necessary and identity-preserving from authorized original-photo references. Artwork illustrates a relationship; it is never empirical evidence.

## Batch acceptance and final exit

A batch passes only when all assigned chapters are complete original prose, its exact claims/sources/cases resolve, the Mosaic incoming/outgoing state is continuous, exercises have answer/assessment intent, figure callouts/accessibility text are present, volatile facts are dated, and authority/non-scope limits remain visible. The Phase 08 parent stays open until cross-volume terminology, claim discipline, originality, provider neutrality, MD continuity, no-invented-outcome, and Volume 1→2 handoff reviews pass.

Phase 08 may create only the manuscript artifacts authorized by its issue. Companion code, publication mutation, generated artwork, PDF, and Abhyaas work require their own accepted scope.
