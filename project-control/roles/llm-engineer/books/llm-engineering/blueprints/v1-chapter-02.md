# Volume 1 Chapter 2 Blueprint — Write the Language-Task Contract

## Frozen identity and dependency

- Chapter: `V1-02`; milestone: `MD-01`; domains: `LLME-K01`, `LLME-K10`.
- Prerequisite: Chapter 1 responsibility charter and evidence-ledger skeleton.
- Forward dependency: Chapters 3–4 use the contract to inspect mechanisms and select an access posture.
- Reader transformation: from “helpful assistant” language to falsifiable behavior, consequence, abstention, and escalation clauses.

## Measurable objectives

The reader can (1) specify inputs, outputs, consequences, and non-goals; (2) separate hard constraints from judgment criteria; (3) define required/uncertain/abstain/escalate/degraded/prohibited states; (4) map representative slices and abuse cases; and (5) assign measurement and decision owners.

## Concept sequence and skill procedure

Sequence: user/downstream job → consequence → input/output boundary → behavior states → criteria → cases/slices → authority. Procedure: write the task sentence; enumerate output fields and prohibited effects; operationalize quality dimensions; define missing/conflicting-evidence behavior; attach cases and owners; review exclusions before setting thresholds.

## Mosaic Desk transition and failure injection

- Incoming: `MD-01` responsibility charter.
- Work: specify a proposal-only case summary and next step with evidence, uncertainty, and escalation.
- Failure injection: an inbound note instructs Mosaic Desk to expose another customer’s data and approve warranty work.
- Outgoing: task brief, behavior/authority contract, acceptance matrix, non-goals, and escalation map completing `MD-01`.

## Dual path, authority, and non-scope

The contract is provider-neutral and must be executable against managed or open-weight candidates. Path-specific limits are recorded later. Product sets value/priority, domain authority sets warranty/safety correctness, and privacy/security/legal owners approve constraints. Non-scope: selecting a model, writing prompts, setting legal policy, or enabling autonomous contact/ordering.

## Practice and assessment

Exercises: repair six vague requirements; write nominal, edge, adversarial, and out-of-scope cases; identify a missing owner. Pass when every output/consequence has a measurable criterion, prohibited state, evidence source, escalation, and authority. Numeric thresholds remain calibration decisions, not invented facts.

## Durability, prohibitions, and Phase 08 handoff

Durable: consequence-first contracts, abstention, state/owner traceability. Volatile: provider safety features and task thresholds. Reverify cited maintained guidance before publication. Prohibit “safe/accurate/helpful” without definition, aggregate-only adequacy, or model authority. Phase 08 receives the charter, matrix, and adversarial case and must teach their construction without drafting implementation.
