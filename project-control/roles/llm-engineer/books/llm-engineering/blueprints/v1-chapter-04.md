# Volume 1 Chapter 4 Blueprint — Select a Model and Access Posture

## Frozen identity and dependency

- Chapter: `V1-04`; milestone: `MD-02`; domains: `LLME-K03`, `LLME-K09`, `LLME-K10`.
- Prerequisite: `MD-01` contract and Chapter 3 mechanism-consequence sheet.
- Forward dependency: Chapters 5–7 freeze the chosen posture behind one provider-neutral boundary.
- Reader transformation: from leaderboard/model-size selection to a measured, reversible responsibility decision.

## Measurable objectives

The reader can (1) compare managed, hosted open-weight, self-hosted, adapted, and hybrid postures; (2) test candidates on common Mosaic cases; (3) identify control, privacy, latency, cost, inspection, lifecycle, and operational obligations; (4) document rejected alternatives; and (5) design a migration seam.

## Concept sequence and skill procedure

Sequence: contract → constraints → candidate access surfaces → comparable behavior evidence → responsibility/cost model → lifecycle risk → decision. Procedure: establish hard gates; run the same frozen cases; record observable/changeable surfaces; assign operations and governance owners; compare tradeoffs; choose a reversible posture with requalification triggers.

## Mosaic Desk transition and failure injection

- Incoming: `MD-02` mechanism risks, cases, privacy/control, response-time, and cost assumptions.
- Work: compare at least one managed and one open-weight candidate without forcing identical internals.
- Failure injection: the highest public benchmark candidate fails residency or typed-output constraints; the preferred managed model also receives a deprecation notice.
- Outgoing: access decision, rejected alternatives, compatibility assumptions, and migration seam completing `MD-02`.

## Dual path, authority, and non-scope

Common request/result/evaluation contracts span both paths. Managed access reduces weight/runtime ownership but adds external lifecycle/data-interface dependencies; open-weight access increases control and packaging/serving duty. Procurement, privacy, security, legal, and platform owners approve their domains. Non-scope: vendor endorsement, infrastructure build, or adaptation decision.

## Practice and assessment

Exercise: fill a weighted evidence table, then invalidate the winner with one hard constraint and produce the next reversible choice. Pass when the record includes comparable cases, limits, owners, migration triggers, and a justified rejection; no leaderboard or parameter count decides alone.

## Durability, prohibitions, and Phase 08 handoff

Durable: common acceptance contract, responsibility split, reversible selection, migration seam. Volatile: names, prices, limits, benchmarks, deprecations. Recheck every dated example. Prohibit “open equals private,” “managed equals safe,” “bigger equals better,” or universal cost claims. Phase 08 receives the decision worksheet, dual-path comparison, and bounded lifecycle case.
