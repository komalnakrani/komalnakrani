## Dual path, authority, and non-scope

Managed baseline and open-weight candidates share protected outcomes; weight-specific diagnostics stay separate. Security/safety/domain owners define unacceptable regressions. Non-scope: safety certification, exhaustive forgetting proof, or changing retrieval during comparison.

## Practice and assessment
