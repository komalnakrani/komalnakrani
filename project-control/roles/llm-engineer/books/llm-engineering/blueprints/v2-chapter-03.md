# Volume 2 Chapter 3 Blueprint — Choose the Smallest Adaptation Ladder

## Frozen identity and dependency

- Chapter: `V2-03`; milestone: `MD-09`; domains: `LLME-K03`, `LLME-K07`, `LLME-K08`, `LLME-K09`.
- Prerequisite: frozen hypothesis and artifact inspection.
- Forward dependency: Chapters 4–8 build only the data/evidence required by the selected candidates.
- Reader transformation: from fashionable method choice to diagnosed, resource-feasible escalation with stop rules.

## Measurable objectives

The reader can compare prompt/context/retrieval repair, SFT, PEFT, preference optimization, distillation, continued pretraining, replacement, and no project; map weight/data/compute/control/reversibility; test simpler alternatives; and select pilots with disconfirmation.

## Concept sequence and skill procedure

Observed gap → responsible layer → lowest intervention → access/resources → predicted effect → protected risks → pilot/rejection. Procedure: classify error; test no-tune repair; map candidate methods to mechanism; eliminate unavailable/unjustified paths; estimate lifecycle burden; predefine accept/reject/stop.

## Mosaic Desk transition and failure injection

- Incoming: audited baseline and model/tokenizer record.
- Failure injection: fine-tuning is proposed to fix changing policy knowledge that belongs in retrieval; half of failures are malformed upstream language codes.
- Outgoing: adaptation decision, rejected alternatives, experiment ladder, access/resource budget, and completed `MD-09` gate.

## Dual path, authority, and non-scope

Managed replacement/no-tune paths remain valid alternatives; weight-changing options require open-weight access. MLOps/platform owns shared infrastructure; research owns novel methods; product/domain own target behavior. Non-scope: optimizer settings, data collection, or “fine-tuning is always better.”

## Practice and assessment

Exercise: Diagnose four failures and defend no-change, repair, PEFT, or reject decisions. Pass when every selected method maps to an evidence-backed mechanism and carries resources, protected risks, and a stop rule.
