## Measurable objectives

The reader can distinguish teacher-to-student transfer from domain/language distribution adaptation; compare SFT/PEFT/replacement/no-project alternatives; audit teacher/corpus provenance; define retention/resource controls; run a bounded pilot; and reject methods whose thesis does not match the gap.

## Concept sequence and skill procedure

Residual gap → behavior/capacity/distribution diagnosis → method fork → teacher/corpus authority → pilot objective/resources → target/retention/control evidence → reject/select. Procedure: restate gap; test simpler paths; identify transfer source; record rights/contamination; cap pilot; compare full evidence; document at least one rejection.

## Mosaic Desk transition and failure injection

- Incoming: preference/SFT/PEFT evidence.
- Failure injection: continued pretraining is proposed for frequently changing policy, or a teacher generates fluent unsupported targets.
- Outgoing: advanced-method decision, bounded pilot or rejection, provenance/resource report, protected results, and completed `MD-13` disposition.
