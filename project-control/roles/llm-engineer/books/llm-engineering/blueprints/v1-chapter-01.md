# Volume 1 Chapter 1 Blueprint — The Model Is Not the Behavior

## Frozen identity and dependency

- Chapter: `V1-01`; milestone: `MD-01`; domains: `LLME-K01`, `LLME-K12`.
- Prerequisite: fictional Mosaic Desk workflow and stakeholder map from the project contract.
- Forward dependency: Chapter 2 converts the responsibility charter into a language-task contract.
- Reader transformation: from judging a fluent sample to locating evidence and authority across a versioned model system.

## Measurable objectives

The reader can (1) draw the model/system/outcome boundary; (2) classify behavior, product, agent, platform, assurance, and domain decisions; (3) replace a demo claim with an evidence question; and (4) assign an accountable owner without transferring formal authority to the model or engineer.

## Concept sequence and skill procedure

Sequence: response observation → configured model behavior → surrounding deterministic system → user/downstream consequence → evidence claim → accountable decision. Procedure: inventory components; mark observable/configurable surfaces; name each proposed claim; attach evidence and limitations; assign technical and formal owners; reject unsupported scope.

## Dual path, authority, and non-scope

Managed and open-weight paths share the same behavior/accountability contract; their observability and operational owners differ. LLM engineering owns model-system behavior evidence. Product owns priority; domain authorities own warranty/safety truth; legal/privacy/security and platform roles retain their mandates. Non-scope: prompt recipes, retrieval, training, autonomous action, legal advice, or safety certification.

## Practice and assessment

Exercise: annotate a misleading demo review with observation, inference, evidence gap, and owner. Pass when the learner produces a charter in which every consequential claim has a criterion, evidence plan, limitation, and authority; no “accurate/helpful/safe” assertion remains undefined.

## Durability, prohibitions, and Phase 08 handoff

Durable: observation/evidence distinction, system boundary, accountable ownership. Volatile: provider interfaces, model names, organizational titles. Reverify any current product examples; never claim a model is accountable, one test proves reliability, or documentation transfers authority. Phase 08 should open with Mosaic Desk’s seductive demo, teach the evidence ledger, and end with the completed charter—fresh prose only, three claims cited, no implementation or image generation.
