# V1 Chapter 1 Research Pack — The Model Is Not the Behavior

## Frozen identity

- Milestone: MD-01, Mosaic Desk intake.
- Purpose: replace demo-driven confidence with a system-behavior accountability frame.
- Reader outcome: distinguish model, configured inference, surrounding application, evaluation evidence, and accountable human authority.

## Evidence questions and claims

1. What can one fluent response establish? `V1-C01-CL01` uses `LLME-BSRC-006`, `007`, and `009`: it is an observation from a configured system, not evidence of dependable behavior.
2. Why start evaluation before optimization? `V1-C01-CL02` uses `007`, `010`, and `011`: lifecycle risk and nondeterministic outputs require explicit evaluation.
3. Where does engineering authority stop? `V1-C01-CL03` uses `008` and `052`: documentation and controls do not transfer legal, safety, product, or domain decision rights.

The durable principle is that behavior is an empirical property of a versioned system under a defined distribution. Provider-specific model names, prices, and benchmark ranks are volatile examples and must not become the chapter's organizing logic.

## Research-backed scenario and case use

Mosaic Desk begins with an attractive email-summary demo. The required failure injection is a small set of multilingual, adversarial, and policy-sensitive messages that reveal omissions and unsafe confidence. Use `LLME-CASE-014` for trust-boundary framing, not as a security implementation guide. The evidence artifact is a system card naming model access, prompts, retrieval/tools, sampling, acceptance criteria, and owners.

## Limits, disputes, and gaps

- NIST sources provide lifecycle and risk vocabulary, not a certification of this workflow.
- Evaluation guidance cannot prove absence of failures; coverage and uncertainty must be visible.
- Model-card practice improves disclosure but does not establish fitness for Mosaic Desk.
- Phase 07 must state who approves product behavior and who handles security/privacy review; the LLM engineer supplies evidence, not those decisions.
