## Evidence defense

For each proposed pattern record: repeated cases; supported task/consequence
scope; artifacts/tests; version dependencies; known failures; disconfirming
evidence; transfer conditions; owner; service boundary; operational burden;
security/safety/domain reviews; retirement trigger; and unresolved uncertainty.
One pilot is an observation, not a platform mandate. A shared service proposal
must identify the platform team's authority and retain application-level effect
semantics.

## FieldOps Relay portfolio review

and document workflow. Expected dispositions can differ: reuse idempotency
ledger pattern, repair memory policy, keep approval application-specific,

platform owner only after evidence. These are blueprint hypotheses, not fixed
answers before exercises run.

## Gaps to keep visible

The evidence base is dominated by provider documentation, standards/protocols,
primary papers and synthetic exercises. It cannot establish enterprise ROI,
legal fitness, safety approval or domain readiness. Those claims require their
own evidence and authorities.
