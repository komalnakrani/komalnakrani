Begin only after Phase 06 issue `#40` is reviewed, committed and closed. Treat
the architecture in commit `b59c3f5` as frozen. Phase 07 may structure chapters;
it may not rename, reorder, merge or split them without reopening Phase 05.

## Mandatory reading order

1. `architecture.md`, especially role boundary, chapter architecture,
   terminology, source conventions and visual rule.
2. `project-map.md` for `AR-01` through `AR-14` artifact continuity.
3. `visual-forecast.md` for figure purpose and later ImageGen raster policy.
4. `source-register.json`, `case-study-register.json`, `claim-register.json` and
   `claim-to-chapter.csv`.
5. The matching `research-packs/chapter-NN.md` before each blueprint.
6. `verification-report.md` for evidence debt and re-verification triggers.

## Blueprint schema required for every chapter

Use these exact fields:

1. frozen title, part, chapter number, `AGE-K*` domains and `AR-*` milestone;
2. professional decision and observable reader capability;
3. prerequisites and five-sentence bridge from the previous dossier state;
4. owned concepts and explicit non-scope/adjacent-role handoffs;
5. ordered section plan with purpose, estimated word range and artifact delta;
6. each `AGE-BCLM-*` claim assigned to a section with source IDs, class,
   limitation, version/date language and disputed treatment;
7. case usage: exact lesson, transferable condition, prohibited generalization
   and FieldOps adaptation;
8. provider-neutral architecture and implementation steps, schemas/pseudocode/
   tests, with optional provider adapters isolated;
9. failure injections and diagnosis sequence;
10. evaluation evidence, acceptance checks and unsupported segments;
11. exercises: input, decision, artifact, observable evidence, expected failure
    and adjacent-role limit;
12. figure anchors from `visual-forecast.md`, learning problem, caption/alt
    obligations and later raster-only ImageGen constraint;
13. chapter summary artifact/version change and exact handoff to next chapter;
14. factual re-verification checklist for protocols, frameworks, models,
    benchmarks and product behavior.
