# Agentic AI Engineer — Book Scope and Volume Decision

## Decision

**SINGLE BOOK**

Publish one coherent mastery book:

> **Agentic AI Engineering**  
> *Designing, Evaluating, and Operating Systems That Act*

## Thesis

Professional agentic engineering is the discipline of converting justified
model-directed autonomy into bounded, inspectable, evaluable, recoverable task
execution across tools, state, people, and production systems.

## Why one book is the smallest coherent structure

The profession has one dependency chain:

`justify autonomy -> contract goal/action/authority -> build loop and tools ->
manage state/delegation -> orchestrate and endure -> evaluate trajectories and
effects -> secure/recover -> operate/release -> evolve`

Splitting construction from evaluation or operation would duplicate the action
contract, harness, state, and authority model. Splitting single-agent from
multi-agent work would overstate multi-agent architecture as a separate
profession. Splitting security into another volume would either impersonate AI
Security/Safety authority or detach controls from the tool and execution
surfaces they protect.

One book lets one fictional system accumulate evidence from initial autonomy
decision through incidents and change. The reference remains substantial, but
its chapters can be used independently after the shared contracts are learned.

- Current role evidence: exact and close titles across multiple employers and

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative A — one lifecycle book

- **Thesis:** engineer delegated action as one controlled production system.
- **Reader transformation:** from competent software/AI engineer to practitioner
  able to build, evaluate, release, and recover a bounded agent system.
- **Project:** one evolving action-and-evidence dossier.
- **Result:** **selected** because contracts, code, evidence, and operational
  decisions stay connected.

### Alternative B — two volumes: build / operate

- Proposed volume 1: loops, tools, state, identity, orchestration.
- Proposed volume 2: evaluation, security, reliability, operation, protocols.
- **Failure:** neither volume can stand alone responsibly. Tool semantics
  require evaluation and recovery; operations require the original state and
  authority contracts. Both would repeat the same system model and capstone.
- **Result:** rejected by the one-sentence-thesis and duplication tests.

### Alternative C — three volumes: single agent / multi-agent / AgentOps

- **Failure:** topology is a design choice, not three independent learning
  professions. It would reward complexity, make multi-agent work look
  compulsory, and detach operations from design.
- **Result:** rejected.

### Alternative D — framework/provider handbooks

- **Failure:** volatile, vendor-shaped, shallow across authority and failure,
  and incompatible with a durable role boundary.
- **Result:** rejected. Provider examples may appear only as versioned evidence
  behind stable concepts and interfaces.

## Domain ownership

The book owns professional depth in:

1. justified autonomy and action contracts;
2. agent loops, harnesses, and state machines;
3. typed tools and effect boundaries;
4. context, state, artifacts, and memory lifecycle;
5. identity propagation, delegation, approvals, and audit;
6. orchestration and handoff semantics;
7. durable execution, cancellation, and recovery;
8. trajectory, state, tool, safety, and outcome evaluation;
9. agent-specific control implementation and human authority;
10. observability, budgets, release, and incident repair;
11. interoperability and change compatibility;
12. evidence standards and earned reuse.

## Non-overlap rules

- **Applied AI Engineer:** this book briefly proves that autonomy is justified,
  then assumes the agent decision and goes deeper on execution. It does not
  reteach the whole AI product lifecycle or non-agentic mechanisms.
- **LLM Engineer:** model behavior, context windows, fine-tuning, and inference
  optimization appear only as inputs to action-system decisions.
- **ML Engineer / research:** no generalized training pipeline, model-serving
  platform, or novel-agent-algorithm curriculum.
- **FDE:** the capstone has stakeholders but does not teach embedded customer
  discovery, commercial scoping, adoption, or engagement handoff.
- **MLOps / platform / SRE:** integrate shared services; do not reproduce their
  generalized infrastructure curricula.
- **Product:** technical recommendation and evidence do not become roadmap,
  prioritization, or business authority.
- **Security / safety / privacy / legal / domain:** implement and test specified
  controls; never claim formal assurance or risk acceptance.

## Audience, prerequisites, and exclusions

### Entry audience

- software, Applied AI, LLM, ML, platform, or automation engineers who can build
  and test production services;
- technical leads responsible for agent systems;
- architects and SRE/security partners who need implementation-depth literacy.

### Prerequisites

- production programming, APIs, schemas, tests, version control, and asynchronous
  failure basics;
- working understanding of LLM inference, prompts, structured outputs, and
  retrieval;
- basic identity/authorization, queues, databases, observability, and deployment;
- ability to reason about user tasks, consequences, and evaluation evidence.

### Explicit exclusions

- beginner programming or LLM introduction;
- framework certification;
- foundation-model training or agent-learning research;
- generalized Kubernetes, cloud, IAM, data engineering, SRE, or security
  curricula;
- legal/compliance guidance or domain authorization;
- speculative artificial general intelligence and autonomous-company design.

## Target size and usability

- 20 chapters in six parts plus appendices.
- Approximately 135,000–155,000 original words, excluding source registers,
  executable code, and generated visual assets.
- One provider-neutral local companion using deterministic model/tool doubles,
  a sandboxed environment, fault injection, and no paid service requirement.
- One capstone dossier plus contrasting satellite cases.
- Selective, purposeful, colorful ImageGen raster visuals; no SVG assets.

The word range controls depth rather than page count. Repetition and framework
catalog material must be removed even if the result is shorter.

## Final merge test

The build and operate halves cannot each sustain a distinct thesis without
repeating the same action, state, authority, and evidence contracts. They
therefore remain one book.

## Phase 05 handoff

Phase 05 may freeze the single-book title, 20-chapter progression, provisional
competency mapping, capstone dossier, source conventions, and raster visual
forecast. Volume count changes only if new evidence demonstrates two genuinely
independent reader transformations.
