# Phase 04 — Agentic AI Engineer Book Scope and Volume Decision

## Objective

Choose the smallest coherent original Komal publishing structure that teaches
Agentic AI Engineering deeply without duplicating Applied AI, LLM, ML, FDE,
platform, SRE, product, or security/safety curricula.

## Inputs

- accepted Phase 01 `PROCEED` verdict
- structured current role evidence and adjacent-role boundary
- existing Agentic AI Engineer manuscripts: none
- deferred Abhyaas competency standard/objective map, recorded as a later
  compatibility check rather than a Komal blocker
- user direction for colorful, fun, crisp ImageGen raster visuals and no SVG

## Work

- Size conceptual, practical, architecture, operational, security/reliability,
  project, prerequisite, and narrative depth for every durable learning cluster.
- Compare at least one book, a build/operate two-volume split, and a
  single-agent/multi-agent/AgentOps larger split.
- Apply the distinct-thesis and independent-reader-transformation tests.
- Lock title/thesis, domain ownership, exclusions, dependencies, and non-overlap.
- Do not split by framework, provider, protocol, or product ambition.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every durable cluster has explicit depth and dependency analysis.
- [ ] One-, two-, and larger-volume alternatives are compared honestly.
- [ ] Each retained volume has a distinct thesis and reader transformation.
- [ ] The decision does not inherit FDE or MLOps volume shape.
- [ ] Applied AI and LLM overlap is resolved explicitly.
- [ ] Security/safety and platform/SRE authority remains outside the book.
- [ ] Target reader, prerequisites, exclusions, project shape, and expected depth
  are credible.
- [ ] The user-directed ImageGen raster/no-SVG production policy is recorded.
- [ ] `books/book-scope-decision.md` is directly actionable for Phase 05.

## Verification

- Re-run the merge test: if proposed volumes repeat goal/action/authority,
  harness, state, evaluation, and recovery contracts, merge them.
- Check every domain against the Phase 01 boundary matrix.
- Confirm no inherited manuscript, case, figure, or other-role structure entered
  the decision.
- Run `git diff --check`.

## Outputs

- `project-control/roles/agentic-ai-engineer/books/book-scope-decision.md`
- updated role state after root acceptance

## Dependencies

- Phase 01 accepted and closed.

## Handoff

Phase 05 may design the accepted structure without reconsidering volume count
unless new evidence invalidates a documented assumption.
