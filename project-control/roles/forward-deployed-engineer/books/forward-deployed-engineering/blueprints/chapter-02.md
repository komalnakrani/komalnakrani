# Chapter 02 Blueprint — Enter the Customer System

## Purpose and exit capability

Teach evidence-disciplined entry into an unfamiliar customer domain. The reader can build a discovery plan, stakeholder/evidence map, minimized access plan, and calibrated evidence log without pretending to domain expertise.

## Prerequisites and non-scope

- Prerequisite: Chapter 1 charter and boundary memo.
- Non-scope: covert research, unrestricted data extraction, legal/privacy advice, domain certification, or treating a sponsor as all users.

## Concepts, skills, and decision models

- Stakeholder dimensions: impact, access, knowledge, responsibility, influence, and decision authority.
- Evidence ladder: reported, observed, measured, reproduced, production; confidence and contradiction tags.
- Access-purpose test: question → minimum evidence → minimum access/data → owner/expiry → handling constraint.
- Skills: interview, observe, triangulate, log uncertainty, identify absent voices, and escalate missing sponsorship/consent.

## Architecture, implementation, and tools

- Architecture content: initial system/data/access inventory with unverified boundaries marked dashed.
- Current tools: interview repository, consent/access ticket, and decision log (`organization-specific/volatile`); provider-neutral Markdown/CSV templates (`durable`). No transcript tool is required.

## Scenario and artifacts

- Orchid movement: plan observation across technicians, dispatch, service operations, inventory, IT, security, data, and regional leadership.
- Complete `OA-01` discovery plan, stakeholder map, access-purpose matrix, evidence log, and escalation path. Reject unrestricted production exports; propose minimized synthetic/redacted or supervised alternatives.

## Cases and bounded use

- `R06-C007`: regulated vendor deployment story illustrates cross-functional collaboration only.
- High-governance satellite: use GOV.UK service guidance as a transfer pattern, not as a claimed commercial process.

## Failures, mistakes, and tradeoffs

- Executive-only discovery; solution-leading questions; storing sensitive evidence without need; confusing access with authority.
- Tradeoff: speed versus breadth of evidence; resolve using consequence, reversibility, missing voice, and a dated gap rather than “complete discovery.”

## Exercise and completion evidence

Given six access requests, minimize or reject each and link retained access to a question. Conduct a mock interview/observation reconciliation. Pass when contradictions remain visible and every access item has owner, purpose, handling, and expiry.
