# Chapter 17 Blueprint — Make the Solution Usable and Ownable

## Purpose and exit capability

The reader can diagnose adoption friction without blaming users and complete an evidence-backed transfer of workflow use, operation, support, release/recovery, access, knowledge, and decision ownership.

## Prerequisites and non-scope

- Prerequisite: stabilized system, current limitations, operating evidence, and target user/support groups.
- Non-scope: broad change management, renewal/account ownership, training as a fix for poor design, document delivery as handoff, or indefinite privileged FDE access.

## Concepts, skills, and decision models

- Adoption-friction taxonomy: capability, workflow fit, trust/evidence, access, performance, incentive, policy, accessibility, support, knowledge.
- Ownership acceptance model: knowledge, access, telemetry, runbook, release, recovery, support, decisions, open risk, change path.
- Demonstration-over-document test; adoption evidence triangulation across use, outcome, support, satisfaction, dropout, and segments.
- Skill: observe task performance, isolate friction, enable operators, and negotiate explicit exceptions/exit.

## Architecture, implementation, and tools

- Product analytics/support/LMS tools (`volatile/organization-specific`) may supply signals; canonical adoption/acceptance records remain portable.
- Companion includes user-evidence display, accessibility checks, runbooks, support event fixtures, role-specific access, and supervised release/recovery tasks.

## Scenario and artifacts

- Complete `OA-10`: adoption review, enablement plan, user/operator docs, support/RACI, access transfer/revocation, supervised customer release/recovery, open-risk exceptions, signed handoff acceptance.
- Diagnose bypass caused by missing recommendation evidence rather than assumed resistance.

## Failures, mistakes, and tradeoffs

- Blame users, train around defects, measure only logins, hand over alerts without access/runbook, retain hidden FDE dependency.
- Tradeoff: engagement exit versus unresolved customer readiness; make residual support and exception explicit rather than disappearing or staying forever.

## Exercise and completion evidence

Diagnose five non-use signals and conduct ownership acceptance. Pass when representative users complete tasks and customer operators execute release/recovery/support paths with required access and evidence.
