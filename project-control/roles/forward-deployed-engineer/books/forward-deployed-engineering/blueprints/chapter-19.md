# Chapter 19 Blueprint — Lead Beyond One Deployment

## Purpose and exit capability

The reader can allocate attention across deployments, communicate evidence at technical/operational/executive altitudes, delegate with review and escalation, and plan capability growth without abandoning engineering depth or claiming adjacent authority.

## Prerequisites and non-scope

- Prerequisite: complete `OA-01`–`OA-11` lifecycle evidence and lessons.
- Non-scope: generic people management, sales leadership, guaranteed title ladder, burnout/travel as competence, or seniority as risk authority.

## Concepts, skills, and decision models

- Portfolio record: outcome/guardrail/adoption trajectory, consequence, evidence gap, time pressure, dependency age, readiness, leverage, next decision, confidence.
- Attention model: consequence × reversibility × evidence gap × decision latency; avoid false numeric precision.
- Delegation contract: objective, authority, evidence, constraints, review points, escalation.
- Multi-altitude memo and capability-evidence growth path.

## Architecture, implementation, and tools

- Portfolio/project tools (`volatile`) can visualize; the decision model and evidence remain portable.
- Companion closes with dossier index, source/build hashes, issue/risk state, reuse status, and next-owner record; no new architecture is introduced.

## Scenario and artifacts

- Finish `OA-11`: portfolio review across Orchid plus two explicitly fictional deployments, escalation memo, operating cadence, staffing/delegation decision, and growth plan.
- Compare migration/availability risk, strong adoption but incomplete guardrails, and a stopped engagement.

## Cases and bounded use

- `R06-C004`: migration/peak-load attention.
- `R06-C006`: headline adoption versus evidence gaps.
- `R06-C010`: final fictional dossier/portfolio exercise.
- Founding-versus-mature-team satellite scenarios are explicitly constructed, not employer outcome claims.

## Failures, mistakes, and tradeoffs

- Loudest-customer priority, numeric heatmap as truth, permanent escalation, delegation without authority, strategy without code/evidence.
- Tradeoff: direct engineering versus leverage/coordination; seniority increases decision quality and system capacity, not distance from evidence.

## Exercise and completion evidence

Prioritize four engagements under incomplete evidence, write executive and technical versions of one escalation, and define a six-month evidence-based growth plan. Pass when choices state consequence, uncertainty, authority, opportunity cost, and review trigger.
