# Chapter 01 Research — The Deployed Outcome

## Research question

What distinguishes FDE outcome ownership from ordinary feature delivery, and where must that ownership stop?

## Claim and evidence map

- **C01.1 — The observed FDE role spans discovery, engineering, rollout, adoption, and feedback into reusable capability.** `R06-S001`, `R06-S002`, `R06-S003`, and `R06-S004` support this as current employer practice. It must not be presented as a statutory or universal definition.
- **C01.2 — The unit of value is an operational outcome across a service/workflow, not software shipment alone.** `R06-S009` supplies a durable service-outcome frame; `R06-S001` and `R06-S004` connect it to present FDE practice.
- **C01.3 — Outcome ownership does not transfer legal, security, safety, domain, or executive authority to the FDE.** `R06-S005`, `R06-S026`, and `R06-S042` support least-required access, explicit policy/approval, and risk ownership boundaries.
- **C01.4 — Product leverage is an explicit part of some current FDE roles.** `R06-S002` supports patterns, playbooks, building blocks, and product feedback. This remains employer evidence.

## Durable principles

Define “done” as measurable, sustained workflow value inside stated guardrails. Separate responsibility for assembling evidence and driving decisions from authority to approve risk. Classify work as FDE core, shared, supporting, or specialist-owned.

## Cases

- `R06-C010` provides the fictional Orchid responsibility charter; it demonstrates boundary reasoning but proves no prevalence.
- `R06-C006` can contrast reported adoption with a complete production-outcome claim: use alone is necessary evidence in many deployments, but it is not sufficient.

## Disputes and limits

FDE, FDSE, solutions engineer, implementation engineer, and professional-services boundaries vary by employer. The chapter can propose a Komal operating model, but must label it as a synthesis. Avoid hero language suggesting unlimited hours, unilateral access, or individual ownership of institutional risk.

## Remaining gaps

No release-blocking gap. Role postings are volatile, so the manuscript must date them and anchor the enduring definition in lifecycle and artifacts rather than company names.

## Manuscript prohibitions

Do not claim every FDE writes production code in the same proportion, owns commercial success, or may bypass formal approvers to achieve an outcome.
