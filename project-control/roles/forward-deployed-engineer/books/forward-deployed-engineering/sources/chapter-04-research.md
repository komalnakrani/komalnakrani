# Chapter 04 Research — Contract for an Outcome

## Research question

How can workflow evidence become measurable success, guardrails, adoption evidence, decision rights, and stop conditions?

## Claim and evidence map

- **C04.1 — Outcome and use measures should be defined early and segmented where aggregate figures conceal user experience.** `R06-S012`.
- **C04.2 — Benefits require a baseline and continuing evidence that can support continue, change, or stop decisions.** `R06-S013`.
- **C04.3 — Satisfaction is one continuous signal and must be interpreted with dropout and workflow evidence.** `R06-S015`.
- **C04.4 — User-relevant service indicators and objectives should connect to actions and tradeoffs.** `R06-S047`.
- **C04.5 — Present FDE descriptions emphasize measurable workflow results and adoption.** `R06-S001` and `R06-S004`; employer/company positioning only.

## Durable principles

An outcome contract names baseline, target direction, measurement window, segment, evidence owner, adoption signal, guardrails, non-goals, assumptions, decision rights, and exit/stop criteria. A metric tree must show how system behavior can influence—but does not automatically cause—the intended outcome.

## Cases

- `R06-C006` supplies an attributed adoption figure; the manuscript must demonstrate why active use is not equivalent to advisor productivity, correctness, or customer benefit.
- `R06-C010` shows a headline closure metric that can improve by excluding difficult tickets. A first-visit-success outcome needs safety, escalation, and cohort guardrails.

## Disputes and limits

Causal attribution may be weak in a rollout without randomization or a credible comparison. Targets are organizational decisions, not numbers the FDE fabricates. Guardrails can conflict with speed or volume goals and require explicit authority.

## Remaining gaps

No release blocker. The blueprint must include a counterfactual/attribution limitations section and prevent false precision in the fictional case.

## Manuscript prohibitions

Do not equate demos, output counts, login counts, or aggregate model accuracy with production outcome. Do not state invented Orchid baselines or targets as measured facts.
