# Chapter 19 Research — Lead Beyond One Deployment

## Research question

How does an FDE allocate attention, communicate evidence, staff and escalate across a portfolio, and grow without losing engineering depth or overstepping authority?

## Claim and evidence map

- **C19.1 — Current FDE practice combines customer outcome work with reusable patterns and cross-functional product/research feedback.** `R06-S001`, `R06-S002`, `R06-S004`; current employer/company evidence.
- **C19.2 — Portfolio judgment should prioritize user-relevant objectives, risk, and explicit action thresholds.** `R06-S047` and `R06-S053`.
- **C19.3 — Operations and improvement practices require documented ownership and continuing review.** `R06-S055`.
- **C19.4 — Cybersecurity and privacy risk ownership remains organizational rather than personal heroics.** `R06-S040`, `R06-S042`.

## Durable principles

Use a portfolio review that shows outcome trajectory, guardrails, adoption, reliability, security/privacy/safety decisions, dependency age, ownership readiness, leverage potential, next decision, and confidence. Allocate attention by consequence and reversibility, not the loudest stakeholder. Delegate artifacts and decisions with authority, evidence, review points, and escalation triggers. Communicate the same facts at technical, operational, and executive altitudes.

## Cases

- `R06-C004` tests attention allocation when a migration risk intersects peak traffic.
- `R06-C006` tests whether a strong adoption headline obscures task or guardrail gaps.
- `R06-C010` ends with portfolio priority, escalation memo, operating cadence, and growth plan grounded in the completed dossier.

## Disputes and limits

Portfolio scoring can create false comparability and Goodhart effects. A senior FDE is not automatically the people manager, product executive, legal approver, or incident commander. Career paths vary by employer and should be presented as capability patterns, not guaranteed levels.

## Remaining gaps

No release blocker. Phase 07 must include a multi-engagement exercise with incomplete evidence and a growth plan tied to observable practices.

## Manuscript prohibitions

Do not reward permanent escalation, normalize burnout/travel as competence, or imply seniority authorizes unilateral customer risk decisions.
