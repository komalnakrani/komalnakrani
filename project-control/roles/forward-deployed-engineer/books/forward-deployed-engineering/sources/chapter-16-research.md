# Chapter 16 Research — Stabilize Under Real Conditions

## Research question

How should an FDE lead technical triage, containment, communication, correction, learning, and stabilization while respecting incident authority?

## Claim and evidence map

- **C16.1 — Incident response should be integrated across organizational cybersecurity risk management.** `R06-S053`.
- **C16.2 — Postmortems can connect impact, detection, causes, decisions, corrective actions, and follow-up through a blameless learning practice.** `R06-S054`.
- **C16.3 — Production monitoring must include data/model quality where workflow behavior depends on it.** `R06-S038`.
- **C16.4 — Metrics, traces, and logs support different diagnostic questions.** `R06-S048`.

## Durable principles

Follow the customer’s incident command. Establish impact, commander, technical leads, communication cadence, decision log, containment options, and escalation first. Separate restoration from durable correction. Define stabilization exit criteria using operating evidence, corrective-action ownership, adoption/support state, and unresolved risk.

## Cases

- `R06-C001`–`R06-C004` provide configuration, demand amplification, access dependency, and migration/capacity incidents.
- `R06-C005` adds non-deterministic model-quality degradation.
- `R06-C010` produces an original incident timeline and correction without inventing a real outcome.

## Disputes and limits

Blameless does not mean accountability-free, evidence-free, or exempt from legal/safety duties. Fast rollback is often appropriate but not universal. Customer communications need an authorized owner; technical confidence must be calibrated as facts change.

## Remaining gaps

No release blocker. Phase 07 must distinguish incident, problem management, and stabilization artifacts without overloading one chapter with specialist forensics.

## Manuscript prohibitions

Do not speculate publicly during an incident, alter evidence casually, hide customer impact, or present first-party postmortems as complete independent truth.
