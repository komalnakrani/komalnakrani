# Chapter 18 Research — Turn Field Evidence Into Product Leverage

## Research question

When does customer-specific delivery evidence justify a reusable component, platform capability, playbook, or product request?

## Claim and evidence map

- **C18.1 — Some present FDE roles explicitly codify deployment patterns, tools, playbooks, building blocks, and product feedback.** `R06-S002`.
- **C18.2 — Current deployment-company and product accounts describe connecting field learning to generalized capability.** `R06-S004`, `R06-S005`; company positioning, not independent outcome evidence.
- **C18.3 — Benefit evidence should support retention, improvement, or stopping.** `R06-S013`.
- **C18.4 — Versioning communicates compatibility only across a declared public contract.** `R06-S028`.
- **C18.5 — Postmortem learning connects evidence to corrective actions rather than folklore.** `R06-S054`.

## Durable principles

Maintain a pattern ledger containing observation, customer context, frequency, variance, consequence, existing workaround, evidence quality, isolation/privacy constraint, candidate leverage, owner, validation plan, and disconfirming evidence. Separate reusable code, configurable capability, reference architecture, playbook, documentation, and product feedback. Abstract only across evidence-supported invariants.

## Cases

- `R06-C007` and `R06-C008` show company-reported production traces informing evaluation and product practices.
- `R06-C009` illustrates a reusable interface pattern with explicit semantic limits.
- `R06-C010` receives a “platformize it” request after one customer; the correct output is a bounded reuse proposal or validation plan, not an invented platform.

## Disputes and limits

Two similar implementations may hide different semantics; one implementation may still reveal a severe product gap. Reuse value must account for maintenance, migration, support, security, and opportunity cost. Customer-derived artifacts and data cannot silently cross isolation or contractual boundaries.

## Remaining gaps

No release blocker. The blueprint must define evidence thresholds as decision prompts, not universal numeric rules.

## Manuscript prohibitions

Do not rename customer code as a platform, expose customer details, or claim market prevalence from a few deployments.
