# Chapter 17 Research — Make the Solution Usable and Ownable

## Research question

What evidence shows that users can perform the workflow and the customer can operate, support, recover, and evolve the deployment without hidden FDE dependency?

## Claim and evidence map

- **C17.1 — Whole-journey evidence includes non-digital paths and broken handoffs.** `R06-S008`, `R06-S009`.
- **C17.2 — Support demand, service levels, and feedback loops belong in the operating model.** `R06-S014`.
- **C17.3 — Satisfaction, dropout, use, and segmented outcome evidence are complementary rather than interchangeable.** `R06-S012`, `R06-S015`.
- **C17.4 — Accessibility criteria provide testable evidence but do not alone prove usability.** `R06-S045`.
- **C17.5 — Launch and cloud-operations guidance emphasizes operability, ownership, recovery, and continuing improvement.** `R06-S052`, `R06-S055`.

## Durable principles

Diagnose adoption as workflow fit, trust/evidence visibility, access, performance, incentive, training, accessibility, policy, or support—not as user resistance by default. Handoff includes code/configuration ownership, runbooks, alerts, support/RACI, access, release/recovery demonstration, open risks, decision history, and explicit acceptance. Documents are inputs to capability, not proof of it.

## Cases

- `R06-C006` provides attributed active-use evidence but cannot establish complete workflow value.
- `R06-C010` injects technicians bypassing recommendations that omit supporting evidence; handoff requires customer engineers to execute a supervised release/recovery.

## Disputes and limits

Training can compensate for knowledge gaps but should not conceal bad workflow design. Use can be coerced by policy and satisfaction can exclude dropouts. A RACI records intended responsibility but cannot prove readiness.

## Remaining gaps

No release blocker. Phase 07 must include adoption diagnosis branches and a hands-on ownership acceptance protocol.

## Manuscript prohibitions

Do not blame users, count document delivery as handoff, or retain privileged access indefinitely because the customer is “not ready.”
