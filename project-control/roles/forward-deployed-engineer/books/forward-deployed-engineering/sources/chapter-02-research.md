# Chapter 02 Research — Enter the Customer System

## Research question

How does an FDE learn an unfamiliar domain and earn legitimate access without pretending to expertise or collecting unnecessary data?

## Claim and evidence map

- **C02.1 — Discovery begins with the whole problem, context, and real users rather than a preferred solution.** `R06-S006`, `R06-S007`, and `R06-S010`.
- **C02.2 — Interviews, observation, existing evidence, and contradiction logs serve different purposes; stakeholder opinion remains an assumption until tested.** `R06-S007`.
- **C02.3 — Access should be tied to a named question and limited to what the work requires.** `R06-S005`, `R06-S026`, and `R06-S042` support least-required access and risk-based treatment.
- **C02.4 — Current FDE practice explicitly combines customer embedding with security/governance collaboration.** `R06-S001` and `R06-S003`; employer-specific evidence only.

## Durable principles

Maintain a stakeholder hypothesis, evidence log, access-purpose map, confidentiality constraints, and decision-rights map. Distinguish reported, observed, inferred, and unresolved statements. Escalate when sponsorship, consent, safe access, or an accountable decision owner is absent.

## Cases

- `R06-C007` shows a company-reported collaboration across customer, research, and deployment teams in a regulated workflow. It does not disclose enough to prescribe the exact discovery process.
- `R06-C010` injects a sponsor request for unrestricted production exports. The correct response is a purpose-bound, minimized data/access plan, not silent compliance.

## Disputes and limits

“Move fast” and “embed deeply” do not override privacy, labor, customer policy, or data residency obligations. The FDE may document a domain expert’s decision but must not impersonate that expert. Research methods must be adapted for accessibility, labor conditions, and user risk.

## Remaining gaps

Jurisdiction-specific privacy and research-consent requirements are intentionally outside book scope and require customer counsel/privacy ownership. No chapter-level release blocker.

## Manuscript prohibitions

Do not recommend unrestricted production access, covert observation, copying sensitive data to personal tools, or treating a sponsor as representative of all users.
