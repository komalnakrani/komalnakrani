1. Does every public claim remain supportable, original, role-specific, and inside the evidence boundary?

5. Does any inherited Alpesh identity/content, fake proof, live certification material, placeholder, generic career copy, or Abhyaas-mandatory language remain?
6. Does the complete role remain usable on representative desktop and mobile layouts without overflow, hidden controls, or unreadable content?
