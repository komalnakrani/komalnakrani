## Comparison contract

The comparison asks for the smallest publication structure that can transform a
reader from someone who can fit and expose a model into an engineer who can
qualify, release, operate, recover, and retire a **versioned learning workload
and its qualification dossier**. The comparison does not inherit the size,
parts, chapter rhythm, examples, or artifact progression of an existing Komal
publication. Working volume labels below describe the decision being tested;
they are not proposed publication titles.

Every alternative is tested against the same rules:

1. preserve the eight durable decisions `LC-01` through `LC-08` rather than
   assigning one publication unit per cluster;
2. preserve 22/22 accepted claims, 17/17 adjacent-role boundaries, and 10/10
   accepted scenarios;
3. give each volume a distinct professional transformation and capstone;
4. merge volumes that must reteach the same task, data, evaluation, release, or
   operations evidence to make their endpoint intelligible;
5. treat an earlier volume's accepted dossier as an input, never as material to
   be retaught in a later volume;
6. reject divisions based on framework, cloud, model family, fashionable
   keyword, or inherited book size; and
7. preserve all `PUB-*`, `ND-*`, `RSV-*`, and `CP-*` guardrails.

### Alternative verdict vocabulary

- **RETAIN — RECOMMENDED:** smallest coherent structure with an independently
  meaningful endpoint and no merge-test failure.
- **RETAIN — FALLBACK:** coherent and independently meaningful, but carries a
  justified seam or production cost that the smaller retained structure avoids.
- **REJECT — MERGE:** one or more volume boundaries cause repeated evidence or
  a dependent endpoint; merge the named units.

## Alternative 1 — `SINGLE BOOK`

### `SB-V1` — end-to-end workload qualification

- **One-sentence thesis:** An MLE's durable craft is to make one learning
  workload's task, data, candidate, qualification, release, operating envelope,
  controls, recovery, and retirement evidence travel together without
  absorbing adjacent owners' authority.
- **Entry prerequisites:** basic supervised-learning concepts; descriptive
  statistics and common metrics; model fitting; tabular/event data; version
  control; tests; schemas and APIs; dependency environments; and basic service,
  observability, and application-security vocabulary. No cloud, framework, or
  model family is required.
- **Professional endpoint:** the reader can lead the technical qualification of
  a versioned learning workload from an ambiguous approved intent through a
  defensible `NO ML`, `HOLD`, `REJECT`, `PASS`, staged release, recovery,
  requalification, or retirement disposition while naming every external
  decision owner.
- **Unique capstone transformation:** transform a promising but underspecified
  candidate into a complete workload dossier, survive a segment regression,
  dependency incompatibility, delayed-label alert, capacity fault, integrity
  requirement, and intended-use change, then execute and prove a controlled
  retirement.
- **Dependencies:** internal evidence flows from `LC-01` to `LC-07`, with
  `LC-08` practiced and assessed across every handoff; domain, product,
  evaluation, platform, SRE, security, safety, privacy, governance, legal, and
  scientific decisions arrive as named external inputs or gates.
- **No-reteaching rule:** prerequisite ML, statistics, software, platform,
  product, security, and domain concepts receive only the minimum literacy
  needed to read a supplied decision or implement a workload-specific control;
  specialist methods are routed to their owner, and each dossier artifact is
  introduced once then revised by traceable change.
- **Cluster allocation:** `LC-01`–`LC-07` form the revisable lifecycle spine;
  `LC-08` is an embedded evidence-and-authority progression rather than a
  detached leadership unit.

**Depth and production shape.** This is the densest individual volume but the
lightest complete structure. Bench Zero orients ownership and evidence. Bench
Setups and Bench Sheets keep each decision legible; Qualification Gates connect
the same artifact identities across changes; the closing dossier proves the
full transformation. Additional screen-first pages are justified only when
they reduce reasoning load, not to mimic the extent of another book.

**Stress result.** `LC-01` cannot disappear into model selection; `LC-02`
cannot collapse into experiment metadata; `LC-04` cannot collapse into metrics;
`LC-05` cannot collapse into a container; `LC-06` cannot collapse into a
dashboard; `LC-07` cannot become launch paperwork; and `LC-08` cannot become
soft-skills advice. A single revisable dossier allows these decisions to stay
distinct without reintroducing their shared evidence at a publication seam.

## Alternative 2 — `2-VOLUME SERIES`

### `2V-V1` — construct and qualify the candidate

- **One-sentence thesis:** A candidate becomes technically meaningful only when
  an approved task contract, admissible data, bounded experiment identity, and
  representative independent qualification agree on what the evidence can and
  cannot support.
- **Entry prerequisites:** basic ML, metrics, probability/statistics, data
  splits and transformations, version control, dependency environments, and
  test literacy.
- **Professional endpoint:** the reader can turn an ambiguous approved intent
  and candidate implementation into a versioned candidate-qualification dossier
  with a baseline, reproducibility bounds, segment evidence, limitations,
  named authorities, and a defensible `NO ML`, `HOLD`, `REJECT`, or technical
  `PASS`.
- **Unique capstone transformation:** start with a celebrated aggregate result;
  expose target ambiguity, leakage, environmental divergence, and a
  consequential segment regression; finish with a repaired or rejected
  candidate and an independently reviewable qualification packet.
- **Dependencies:** `LC-01` supplies authority and acceptance; `LC-02` supplies
  admissible identities; `LC-03` supplies candidate evidence; `LC-04` makes the
  technical disposition; `LC-08` makes handoffs and stop-ship voices explicit.
- **No-reteaching rule:** teach no serving, fleet, shared pipeline, generalized
  evaluation, product, or research curriculum; record release and operating
  requirements only as unresolved handoff fields, not as substitute depth.
- **Cluster allocation:** primary `LC-01`–`LC-04`; embedded `LC-08`; only
  forward references to the evidence that `LC-05`–`LC-07` will later consume.

### `2V-V2` — release, operate, recover, and retire the workload

- **One-sentence thesis:** A qualified candidate becomes a dependable learning
  workload only when its executable package, compatibility, serving envelope,
  observed behavior, controls, recovery, requalification, and retirement state
  remain bound to the accepted dossier.
- **Entry prerequisites:** the accepted `2V-V1` candidate-qualification dossier
  or an equivalent diagnostic gate proving `LC-01`–`LC-04` artifacts; plus API,
  packaging, dependency, service, observability, capacity, incident, and basic
  security literacy.
- **Professional endpoint:** the reader can assemble, stage, observe, diagnose,
  recover, requalify, and retire one workload under named MLOps/platform/SRE/
  security/formal authority while preserving exact lineage and a previous-good
  state.
- **Unique capstone transformation:** turn the accepted Volume 1 dossier into a
  compatible signed release bundle; stage it through a capacity and dependency
  fault; resolve an ambiguous delayed-label alert; prove rollback and
  requalification; and close with verified non-serving retirement evidence.
- **Dependencies:** consumes, but never reconstructs, the frozen Volume 1 task,
  data, candidate, and gate identities; applies `LC-05`–`LC-07`; extends
  `LC-08` from one-workload handoffs to cross-team recovery and reusable
  workload standards.
- **No-reteaching rule:** begin with a dossier conformance check rather than a
  qualification recap; any invalidated task/data/evaluation evidence routes the
  reader back to Volume 1 or an equivalent gate. Do not teach generalized
  deployment platforms, SLO programs, pipelines, security policy, or formal
  authorization.
- **Cluster allocation:** primary `LC-05`–`LC-07`; embedded advanced `LC-08`;
  `LC-01`–`LC-04` appear only through versioned consumed evidence and explicit
  re-open triggers.

**Stress result.** Both endpoints are professionally meaningful: Volume 1 ends
with a qualification decision that can reject or hold a candidate, while
Volume 2 ends with lifecycle accountability in production. The structure passes
only if Volume 2's entry gate makes prior artifacts immutable inputs. If Volume
2 explains task framing, data validation, experiment construction, or
representative evaluation again, the task/data/evaluation merge tests force the
two volumes back into `SINGLE BOOK`.

## Alternative 3 — `3-VOLUME SERIES`

### `3V-V1` — contract the evidence and reconstruct the candidate

- **One-sentence thesis:** A learning candidate is inspectable only when its
  task authority, data identities, retained baseline, run context, and bounded
  reproducibility claim are explicit.
- **Entry prerequisites:** basic ML, data handling, descriptive statistics,
  version control, dependency environments, and tests.
- **Professional endpoint:** the reader can produce a reproducible-within-bounds
  candidate evidence packet and expose whether the task, data, or experiment is
  too weak to proceed.
- **Unique capstone transformation:** convert an underspecified experiment into
  a versioned task/data/run packet, reproduce it across two environments, and
  document the strongest supportable claim plus unresolved authority.
- **Dependencies:** `LC-01`–`LC-03`, with `LC-08` handoff literacy; qualification
  sufficiency remains unresolved for Volume 2.
- **No-reteaching rule:** do not teach independent evaluation design, release,
  or operations; stop at candidate evidence and route statistical, data, and
  scientific authority.
- **Cluster allocation:** primary `LC-01`–`LC-03`; embedded foundation-level
  `LC-08`.

### `3V-V2` — qualify and bind the release

- **One-sentence thesis:** Candidate evidence becomes releasable only when a
  representative technical disposition is inseparably bound to an executable,
  compatible, lineage-complete package and previous-good target.
- **Entry prerequisites:** the accepted `3V-V1` task/data/run packet or an
  equivalent diagnostic gate; metrics, uncertainty, calibration where
  applicable, schemas, APIs, packaging, and testing literacy.
- **Professional endpoint:** the reader can defend a segment-aware technical
  disposition and assemble the exact release bundle that the disposition
  covers, without self-authorizing deployment.
- **Unique capstone transformation:** repair or reject a candidate after a
  segment conflict, then bind the accepted candidate to interfaces,
  dependencies, lineage, compatibility evidence, limitations, and rollback
  identity.
- **Dependencies:** consumes `LC-01`–`LC-03`; teaches `LC-04`–`LC-05`; embeds
  intermediate `LC-08`; produces the release identity consumed by Volume 3.
- **No-reteaching rule:** verify the prior evidence manifest without rebuilding
  it; do not teach a generalized evaluation program, application composition,
  registry service, or delivery pipeline.
- **Cluster allocation:** primary `LC-04`–`LC-05`; embedded intermediate
  `LC-08`.

### `3V-V3` — operate, govern, recover, and retire

- **One-sentence thesis:** A released workload remains qualified only when its
  measured envelope, signals, controls, incident decisions, requalification,
  formal authority, and retirement proof stay connected to the exact release.
- **Entry prerequisites:** the accepted `3V-V2` release bundle and gate record
  or an equivalent diagnostic gate; service, observability, capacity, incident,
  and application-security literacy.
- **Professional endpoint:** the reader can operate, contain, roll back,
  requalify, and retire a workload under explicit shared-system and formal
  authority.
- **Unique capstone transformation:** stage the Volume 2 release through load,
  dependency, delayed-label, and integrity failures; execute recovery; respond
  to an intended-use change; and prove retirement.
- **Dependencies:** consumes the complete Volumes 1–2 dossier; teaches
  `LC-06`–`LC-07`; embeds advanced `LC-08`.
- **No-reteaching rule:** begin from exact release and gate identities; route
  any invalidated input back to its owning evidence gate; do not teach fleet
  SLOs, incident command, platform operation, security policy, or regulatory
  interpretation.
- **Cluster allocation:** primary `LC-06`–`LC-07`; embedded advanced `LC-08`.

**Stress result.** Each local endpoint names real work, but the publication
structure fails the merge discipline. Volume 2 must explain enough task/data/
run evidence to make qualification defensible, while Volume 3 must explain
enough evaluation/release evidence to make requalification defensible. The
release boundary itself spans `LC-04`–`LC-06`; splitting it requires repeated
gate, limitation, lineage, and rollback explanations. `LC-08` also cannot be
deferred, yet three separate progressions risk becoming repeated authority
orientation. The extra seam creates no new accountability center, so the
structure must merge to one or two volumes.

## Alternative 4 — `4+-VOLUME SERIES`

The strongest version of the `4+` family is tested at four volumes. Adding a
fifth or later unit can only isolate a context, tool, specialist role, or one of
the same cross-cutting decisions; no ninth durable MLE responsibility exists in
the accepted evidence.

### `4V-V1` — task and data contracts

- **One-sentence thesis:** A workload is admissible only when purpose,
  population, consequences, decision rights, data, labels, features, feedback,
  and conformance are versioned and reviewable.
- **Entry prerequisites:** basic ML, descriptive statistics, data querying,
  schemas, transformations, and tests.
- **Professional endpoint:** the reader can issue a defensible task/data
  readiness disposition and route missing product, data, domain, or formal
  authority.
- **Unique capstone transformation:** turn an ambiguous request and leaky data
  snapshot into a versioned task/data contract with a retained no-ML baseline
  and explicit hold conditions.
- **Dependencies:** `LC-01`–`LC-02`, with foundation-level `LC-08`.
- **No-reteaching rule:** stop before experiment and evaluation methods; never
  become product discovery, enterprise data engineering, or governance policy.
- **Cluster allocation:** primary `LC-01`–`LC-02`; embedded `LC-08`.

### `4V-V2` — experiment and qualification

- **One-sentence thesis:** A candidate deserves a technical disposition only
  when bounded reproducibility and representative evaluation challenge its
  claimed improvement.
- **Entry prerequisites:** an accepted `4V-V1` contract, model fitting, metrics,
  uncertainty, dependency environments, and tests.
- **Professional endpoint:** the reader can produce an independently reviewable
  candidate qualification with limitations and a `HOLD`, `REJECT`, or technical
  `PASS`.
- **Unique capstone transformation:** reconstruct a promising run, expose
  environmental divergence and a segment regression, and defend the resulting
  disposition.
- **Dependencies:** consumes `LC-01`–`LC-02`; teaches `LC-03`–`LC-04`; embeds
  intermediate `LC-08`.
- **No-reteaching rule:** consume contract identities without rebuilding them;
  route scientific claims and independent gate design.
- **Cluster allocation:** primary `LC-03`–`LC-04`; embedded `LC-08`.

### `4V-V3` — release and operation

- **One-sentence thesis:** A qualified candidate becomes supportable only when
  its executable bundle, compatibility, serving envelope, observation,
  rollback, and requalification evidence are one operating unit.
- **Entry prerequisites:** an accepted `4V-V2` gate and candidate identity;
  schemas, APIs, packaging, service, observability, capacity, and incident
  literacy.
- **Professional endpoint:** the reader can package, stage, diagnose, contain,
  roll back, and requalify a workload under shared platform and SRE authority.
- **Unique capstone transformation:** bind the candidate to a release, survive a
  compatibility and capacity fault plus a misleading drift signal, and prove
  recovery to a previous-good state.
- **Dependencies:** consumes `LC-01`–`LC-04`; teaches `LC-05`–`LC-06`; embeds
  operational `LC-08`.
- **No-reteaching rule:** do not rebuild candidate qualification; do not teach
  generalized software, MLOps, platform, observability, SLO, or incident-command
  systems.
- **Cluster allocation:** primary `LC-05`–`LC-06`; embedded `LC-08`.

### `4V-V4` — controls, authority, retirement, and widening impact

- **One-sentence thesis:** Long-lived accountability requires workload controls,
  external authorization, lifecycle reassessment, retirement proof, and
  cross-team evidence standards that never erase specialist ownership.
- **Entry prerequisites:** the complete `4V-V1` through `4V-V3` dossier; basic
  application security, data lifecycle, review, and technical-writing literacy.
- **Professional endpoint:** the reader can implement and prove approved
  controls, route formal decisions, close the workload lifecycle, and propose a
  reusable workload evidence standard without seizing policy or platform
  authority.
- **Unique capstone transformation:** trace an integrity challenge and
  intended-use change across the full dossier, obtain separately recorded
  dispositions, retire the workload, and extract one bounded reusable standard.
- **Dependencies:** `LC-07` requires artifacts from every earlier cluster;
  advanced `LC-08` requires credible depth across `LC-01`–`LC-07`.
- **No-reteaching rule:** consume the full dossier and supplied control/authority
  requirements; do not teach security architecture, assurance, safety,
  governance, law, domain validation, management, or enterprise architecture.
- **Cluster allocation:** primary `LC-07`; synthesizing `LC-08`; all earlier
  clusters necessarily appear as evidence inputs.

**Stress result.** The fourth endpoint cannot stand alone without replaying the
task, data, evaluation, release, and operations evidence whose controls and
retirement it closes. Moving security/authority into earlier volumes removes
the fourth volume's unique transformation; deferring them makes Volumes 1–3
unsafe. Likewise `LC-08` is a progression through all decisions, not a
stand-alone leadership book. A fifth volume would have to split by context,
tool, model family, seniority label, fashionable keyword, or inherited size,
all of which are prohibited and add no accepted MLE accountability. The entire
`4+` family therefore fails and must merge.
