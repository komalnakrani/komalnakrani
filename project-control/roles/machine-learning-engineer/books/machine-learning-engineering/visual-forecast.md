## Production doctrine

### Learning and assessment

Every chapter opens with **Bench Setup**, makes the working artifact inspectable
on a **Bench Sheet**, and ends at a **Qualification Gate**. A valid gate records
input identity, measured evidence, limitation, authority owner, one of
`PASS|HOLD|REJECT|REOPEN`, and next evidence. Recall, scenario classification,
artifact construction, deterministic companion work, failure injection,
authority routing, port transfer, and hostile review are used only when they
test the chapter's named decision. A score without its evidence record is not
accepted evidence.

`PLANNED EXECUTED SYNTHETIC` below means a later phase must execute the named
offline companion check over fixed synthetic fixtures and record actual output.
It does not say that an execution has already occurred. `ILLUSTRATIVE` material
must be visibly labeled and cannot satisfy a gate. Nothing in a constructed case
implies production, industrial, safety, financial, business, or provider
performance.

### Source requirements, not sources

Phase 06 must fill the source register from current primary, official,
standards, or original-research material appropriate to each claim. Every
retained source record must include source ID, claim ID, chapter use, access
date, limitation, volatility, and case-truth role. Durable doctrine must remain
valid when vendor, product, API, price, model family, benchmark, title, and
workflow names change. Volatile mechanisms require a current access date, a
bounded mechanism note, and an edition-refresh flag. No source may be stretched
beyond the proposition it supports.

Existing Komal books and supplied design-reference books are not technical
evidence. Employer postings are not technical authority. Constructed cases do
not prove doctrine. Unlabeled synthetic or illustrative results fail. Vendor
documentation may establish a current mechanism, but not workload
qualification, formal authorization, or independent assurance.

### Exact visual truth and selective raster art

Exact plots, axes, thresholds, tables, identifiers, labels, legends, and
overlays remain semantic HTML/CSS so the information is selectable, searchable,
zoomable, and screen-reader available. Raster art may reinforce a spatial or
causal relationship but never contains irreplaceable numeric truth, paragraphs,
code, dense labels, or pseudo-text. There is no image quota.

Only four chapters currently clear the ImageGen threshold: `MLE-CH-05`,
`MLE-CH-14`, `MLE-CH-16`, and `MLE-CH-18`. They are candidates, not approved
assets. Phase 10 may remove any candidate when manuscript context shows the
semantic treatment is sufficient. It may not add a decorative image merely to
balance chapter counts. Canonical generated art is PNG only; Astro owns delivery
optimization. No stored SVG or WebP publication asset may be created.

Every retained generated figure must have: figure ID and version; learning
problem; exact relationship; verified short-label list; facts and source IDs;
caption; alt text; long-description disposition; approved prompt; avoid list;
pixel dimensions and aspect ratio; canonical `.png` path; ImageGen disclosure;
creation date; SHA-256; technical, accessibility, crop, small-size,
originality, and publication review dispositions. Color is always redundant
with label, position, shape, line style, or texture.

### Canonical reopen trigger table

This is the exact lifecycle mapping accepted in the architecture. Every chapter
`REOPEN` outcome applies this table; a chapter cannot invent, rename, collapse,
or bypass a trigger, target, or invalidation.

| Exact change trigger | Exact reopen target | Exact invalidates list |
| --- | --- | --- |
| purpose or intended use | `CONTRACTED` | task; population; acceptance; qualification; release |
| data, labels, features, or population | `ADMISSIBLE` | input conformance; experiment; qualification; release |
| runtime, dependencies, interface, or serving envelope | `RECONSTRUCTIBLE` | run identity; package; compatibility; operating evidence |
| authority, constraint, or permitted use | `CONTRACTED` | approvals; controls; technical disposition; release |

Before a chapter or figure advances, verify:

1. **Learning:** the artifact tests its named decision and measurable objective.
2. **Evidence:** inputs, measured evidence, limitation, owner, disposition, and next evidence all resolve.
3. **Truth:** executed/recorded/illustrative/synthetic state is explicit; case and claim limits are visible.
4. **Source:** every doctrine/mechanism claim has a correctly scoped requirement; durable and volatile treatment is explicit.
5. **Authority:** the MLE ceiling and external decision owner remain visible.
6. **Port transfer:** the same decision/evidence interface works across all five ports.
7. **Precision:** exact identities, axes, thresholds, values, and overlays remain semantic HTML/CSS.
8. **Accessibility:** reading order, headers, labels, contrast redundancy, caption, alt, and long-description disposition pass.
9. **Raster restraint:** a generated candidate materially improves a named relationship, contains no irreplaceable truth, and passes its prompt/avoid contract.
10. **Integrity:** PNG-only canonical path, provenance fields, dimensions, hash, originality, crop, and small-size review are complete before retention.
11. **Page inflation:** removal must reduce comprehension, decision quality, accessibility, or traceability; otherwise remove the element/page.
12. **Boundary:** no stored SVG/WebP, fake UI, logo, watermark, generic mascot, embedded paragraph, decorative quota, copied reference design, or unapproved Komal likeness.

## Phase handoff

Phase 06 receives chapter-specific research requirements and case limitations,
not a preselected bibliography. Phase 07 receives the 21 measurable learning
contracts and exact evidence exits. Phase 08 receives the page grammar and
semantic-visual decisions. Phase 09 receives figure/accessibility/source-record
requirements. Phase 10 receives only the four candidate prompt specifications
and may generate nothing until source and manuscript context validate the
learning need. Later phases may improve execution while preserving
`CP-01`–`CP-14`, author truth, case truth, the five-port invariant, the exact
closing statement, and the prohibition on unsupported authority or results.
