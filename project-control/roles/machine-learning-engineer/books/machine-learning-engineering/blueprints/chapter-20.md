## Skill procedure and evidence interpretation

1. Freeze the serving-path inventory across endpoints, routes, aliases, consumers, credentials, packages, registries, monitors, fallbacks, devices, and recovery copies.
2. Assign disable, revoke, remove, retain, archive, sanitize, preserve, or recheck actions with distinct owners and approval evidence.
3. Execute bounded negative probes for testable paths and record observation time, expected denial, observed result, and limitation.
4. Inject one residual path per port, including delayed or disconnected consumers, and refuse a global non-serving claim.
5. Preserve immutable evidence and authorized recovery material while keeping retention and sanitization decisions external.
6. Issue RETIRED only for the enumerated inventory and schedule named rechecks for paths that cannot be observed now.

- **Strongest supportable conclusion:** No serving route was observed across the exact inventoried and testable workload paths at the recorded time; delayed paths carry explicit rechecks and the conclusion is not global.
- **Counterexample:** The primary endpoint is deleted, but a registry alias, credential, fallback consumer, or disconnected edge device can still execute the package.
- **Limitation:** Negative probes cannot establish an unknowable global negative, and the book cannot decide legal retention, sanitization, or destruction requirements.
