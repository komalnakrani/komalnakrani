# Whole-book furniture blueprint

This screen-first production contract covers the complete opening, navigation, appendix, closing, and author experience for Komal Nakrani’s *Machine Learning Engineering: From Task Contract to Operating Evidence*. It is functional book architecture: every item routes a reader, exposes evidence, preserves authority, or closes the dossier.

## Opening contract

Ten Bench Zero items establish title, prerequisites, ownership, evidence states, Bench Sheet grammar, routed contents, case truth, and the seven-part map before Chapter 01.

## Part navigation and qualification gates

Each of the seven part records contains the exact five obligations: Bench Setup, part decision question, incoming evidence, part map, and part-exit Qualification Gate.

## Appendices and closing dossier

Seven appendices preserve schemas, evidence vocabulary, port transfer, authority, case truth, sources, visuals, accessibility, and edition history. Five closing items include the terminal dossier, reusable standard, systemic escalation, final review, and About Komal.
