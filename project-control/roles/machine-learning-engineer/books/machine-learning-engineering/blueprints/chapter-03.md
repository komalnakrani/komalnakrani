- Produce BL-02 without transferring tolerance-setting to the MLE.

## Skill procedure and evidence interpretation

1. Freeze the incumbent executable, inputs, and measurement contract.
2. Run the incumbent and retain raw segment-level evidence.
3. Map each measure to purpose, condition, population segment, and named consequence.
4. Record proposed target, threshold, accepting owner, status, and unresolved assumption separately.
5. Reject aggregate-only conclusions and issue BL-02 with limitations.

- Strongest supportable conclusion: A baseline becomes decision evidence only when it is runnable under the candidate contract and tied to named consequences.
- Counterexample: A five-point aggregate gain is not acceptable when the high-consequence low-light segment breaches its owner-accepted floor.
- Limitation: The MLE can measure and expose thresholds but cannot determine acceptable harm or business tradeoffs alone.
