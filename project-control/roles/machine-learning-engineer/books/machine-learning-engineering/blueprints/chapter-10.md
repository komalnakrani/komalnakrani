## Bench Setup, Bench Sheet, and Qualification Gate

### Bench Setup

- Decision: Does the candidate satisfy consequence-linked segment gates when the aggregate improves?
- Inputs: `BL-08` plus canonical mappings and offline fixtures.
- Failure pressure: The average improves while a protected or high-consequence segment regresses.

### Bench Sheet

| decision | evidence | state and dossier delta | owner | authority route | next evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Does the candidate satisfy consequence-linked segment gates when the aggregate improves? | population card and consequential-segment suite and failure trace | `CANDIDATE` → `CANDIDATE` via `BL-09` | MLE compiles workload evidence | Evaluation owns suite adequacy; product/domain/formal authorities own acceptable consequences. | BL-10 uncertainty and failure evidence |

### Qualification Gate

- PASS: identities, evidence, limitations, owner routes, ports, and acceptance checks resolve.
- HOLD: required evidence or external decision is missing or a recoverable failure remains.
- REJECT: the candidate basis cannot satisfy the named contract.
- REOPEN: a frozen lifecycle trigger invalidates scope.
- Illegal: automatic promotion, hidden failure, silent upstream repair, or self-approval.

## Skill procedure and evidence interpretation

1. Name population, conditions, exclusions, consequences, and owners.
2. Build the population card and segment matrix before reading the aggregate.
3. Calculate exact aggregate and segment values from fixed fixtures.
4. Apply owner-approved thresholds and sparse-cell rules.
5. Separate CASE-09 reported facts from transfer inference.
6. PASS only when required cells and owner routes resolve.

- **Strongest supportable conclusion:** Support extends only to named population and segment cells meeting consequence-linked gates.
- **Counterexample:** Aggregate accuracy rises while a high-consequence segment regresses.
- **Limitation:** No generic population list or historical audit supplies universal thresholds.
