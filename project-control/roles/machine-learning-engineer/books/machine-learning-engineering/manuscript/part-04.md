# Part 4 — Stress and Qualify: Earn a Disposition

## Bench Setup

The candidate in `BL-08` is deliberately placed under pressure. Aggregate
scores are split by consequential population and segment; uncertainty or
calibration is evaluated where relevant; failure families and sample limits are
made visible. The output is a technical disposition with owners and
limitations, never an automatic launch.

## Part decision question

**Does the evidence support `PASS`, `HOLD`, or `REJECT` for the named
population, segments, and consequences?**

The decision is conjunctive. A favorable mean cannot waive a consequential
segment, missing owner, invalid method, unresolved limitation, or rollback gap.

## Incoming evidence

`BL-08` binds the nominated candidate, incumbent, comparison procedure, run
identity, observed failures, and limitations. The qualification suite must
remain independent of the nomination logic and within the contracted intended
use.

## Part map

- [Chapter 10](chapter-10.md) writes `BL-09`, the population and segment gate
  with consequence-linked precedence.
- [Chapter 11](chapter-11.md) writes `BL-10`, adding uncertainty, calibration
  relevance, error taxonomy, sample limits, and unresolved evidence.
- [Chapter 12](chapter-12.md) combines those records into `BL-11`, explicitly
  producing `TECHNICALLY-QUALIFIED`, `HOLD`, or `REJECT`.

## Part-exit Qualification Gate

Managed, classical, deep, edge, and shared ports must transfer population,
segment, uncertainty, failure, authority, and disposition evidence with the
same shape. The method may vary, but consequence-linked thresholds and named
limitations cannot disappear behind provider reports or model-specific tools.

`PASS` means only `TECHNICALLY-QUALIFIED` inside the task contract. `HOLD`
preserves the candidate until a named gap is resolved and independently
reviewed. `REJECT` preserves the failed record and blocks downstream release.
The MLE cannot waive a consequential gate or supply an external approval. Only
`BL-11:TECHNICALLY-QUALIFIED` may enter [Part 5](part-05.md); `HOLD →
RELEASABLE` and `REJECT → RELEASABLE` are forbidden transitions.
