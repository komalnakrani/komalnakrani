# Part 7 — Control and Close: Preserve Evidence Through the End

## Bench Setup

The workload enters as `REQUALIFIED` or `ROLLED-BACK` with `BL-17`. The final
part consolidates workload controls, exceptions, residual limitations, and
formal decisions; retires every known consumer, endpoint, credential, monitor,
registry alias, recovery copy, and delayed device path; then hostile-reviews the
complete dossier. Retirement is an observed, time-bounded proposition.

## Part decision question

**Can accumulated controls, formal decisions, retirement, and non-serving
evidence withstand hostile review?**

The answer must preserve actual owner decisions and failed evidence. An
inventory row marked complete without a negative probe, observation window, or
owner is not non-serving proof.

## Incoming evidence

`BL-17` supplies containment, rollback or requalification, changed identity,
and incident limitations. Chapters 19–21 also inspect the earlier task,
qualification, release, and recovery records; the close cannot be inferred from
the last runtime state alone.

## Part map

- [Chapter 20](chapter-20.md) writes `BL-19`, recording bounded non-serving

## Part-exit Qualification Gate

Managed, classical, deep, edge, and shared ports transfer controls, formal
decisions, retirement actions, non-serving probes, and the reusable-standard
proposal. Provider deletion, file removal, checkpoint deregistration, device
withdrawal, or tenant disablement is only one mechanism; each known endpoint,
alias, credential, consumer, cache, monitor, recovery copy, and delayed path
must have an owned record.

`PASS` requires a hash-valid dossier, legal transitions, resolvable decisions,
bounded retirement evidence, retained limitations, and no self-approval.
`HOLD` preserves an unobserved or unreachable path for later proof. `REJECT`
records a failed close and keeps containment active. `REVIEWED` is terminal for
this dossier, not automatic organizational adoption, certification, or release.
