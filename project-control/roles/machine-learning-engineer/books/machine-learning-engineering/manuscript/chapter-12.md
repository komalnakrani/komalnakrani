## MLE-CH-12-S06 — Five-port transfer

For Issue a Technical Qualification Disposition, port parity is tested by asking whether the disposition survives a change of mechanism. The rows below are mechanically distinct and disposition-equal; no port is the reference implementation.

### PORT-CLASSICAL
