# Appendix E — Case truth and transfer register

Every case has a truth label. A constructed trace may demonstrate mechanics but
has no reported facts or outcomes. A public case may contribute only the facts
and attributed outcomes stated by its sources, within their date and method
limits. Transfer means applying a decision job to local evidence; it does not
mean predicting a local outcome.

## Public-case reading rule

For `CASE-06` through `CASE-12`, preserve five separate fields: reported facts,
attributed outcomes, allowed inference, forbidden inference, and limitations.
Quantities keep their source date. The Uber 2017 and 2025 reports remain
separate observations. Gender Shades remains bounded to its historical audit.
Knight Capital's reported deployment failure supports control and retirement
questions, not an ML-specific failure rate.

## Transfer test

A transfer is admissible only when it names the local decision, local evidence,
local owner, and reason the case pattern is relevant. It must also name at least
one reason the case may not transfer. Never copy a source's metric, threshold,
architecture, outcome, or disposition into the Benchline dossier. Consult
[Appendix F](appendix-f.md) for source currentness and the chapter's own Bench
Sheet for the permitted placement.
