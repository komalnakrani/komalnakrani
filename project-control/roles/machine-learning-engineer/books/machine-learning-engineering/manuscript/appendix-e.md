# Appendix E — Case truth and transfer register

Every case has a truth label. A constructed trace may demonstrate mechanics but
has no reported facts or outcomes. A public case may contribute only the facts
and attributed outcomes stated by its sources, within their date and method
limits. Transfer means applying a decision job to local evidence; it does not
mean predicting a local outcome.

| Case | Truth label | Allowed transfer | Forbidden inference and limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CASE-01` Benchline Inspection Dossier | FICTIONAL SYNTHETIC CAPSTONE | Carry one edge workload through the complete record and failure-injection grammar | No real production, industrial, safety, performance, or business claim |
| `CASE-02` Managed demand forecast | CONSTRUCTED SATELLITE | Test exportable evidence and provider-opacity pressure | Not a cloud-provider tutorial; no real demand outcome |
| `CASE-03` Classical credit triage | CONSTRUCTED SATELLITE | Test feature order, calibration/segment evidence, decision consequence, and retirement | Not financial advice or credit authorization |
| `CASE-04` Deep acoustic event classifier | CONSTRUCTED SATELLITE | Test checkpoint, preprocessing, accelerator context, package, and serving transfer | Not a deep-learning recipe and no real acoustic/safety result |
| `CASE-05` Shared ranking-platform tenant | CONSTRUCTED SATELLITE | Separate tenant evidence from shared-platform and fleet authority | Not a platform curriculum; no platform-wide assurance |
| `CASE-06` Google ML Test Score method | PUBLIC REPORTED CASE | Use a multi-layer test inventory to expose model-only review gaps and justify a release-critical `HOLD` | No universal score or threshold; historical Google experience is not independent evidence elsewhere |
| `CASE-07` Uber Michelangelo reports | PUBLIC REPORTED CASE | Transfer stable feature, transform, input, run, conformance, and shared-mechanism evidence patterns | First-party 2017 and 2025 reports are not an independent audit and must not be collapsed into one timeless architecture |

## Public-case reading rule

For `CASE-06` through `CASE-12`, preserve five separate fields: reported facts,
attributed outcomes, allowed inference, forbidden inference, and limitations.
Quantities keep their source date. The Uber 2017 and 2025 reports remain
separate observations. Gender Shades remains bounded to its historical audit.
Knight Capital's reported deployment failure supports control and retirement
questions, not an ML-specific failure rate.

## Transfer test

A transfer is admissible only when it names the local decision, local evidence,
local owner, and reason the case pattern is relevant. It must also name at least
one reason the case may not transfer. Never copy a source's metric, threshold,
architecture, outcome, or disposition into the Benchline dossier. Consult
[Appendix F](appendix-f.md) for source currentness and the chapter's own Bench
Sheet for the permitted placement.
