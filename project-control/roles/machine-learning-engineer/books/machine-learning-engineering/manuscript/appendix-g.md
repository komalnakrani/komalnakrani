# Appendix G — Visual, accessibility, and edition record

All decision and numeric truth in this manuscript is selectable semantic text.
Tables have headers and logical reading order. State rails spell out every state
and transition. Labels, line style, position, and text carry meaning so that
color is never the sole encoding. Figures belong after their first textual
reference, with captions that name both the decision supported and the
limitation.

## Semantic visual register

Twenty-five visual records support the twenty-one chapters. Twenty-one are
selectable tables, matrices, rails, or decision diagrams. Four optional spatial
figures have one edition-neutral contract. When an edition includes one of
these figures, its canonical form is a 2400 × 1600, 3:2 PNG with ImageGen-only
provenance; SVG and WebP are not canonical substitutes. The raster never owns
numeric truth.

All four contracts prohibit quantitative truth, logos, watermarks, fake UI,
generated paragraph text, SVG, and WebP. Their short labels orient the spatial
relationship; the adjacent selectable text carries exact identifiers,
conditions, values, and limitations.

## Alt text and long descriptions

Alt text states the figure's decision purpose and essential relationship; it
does not repeat the caption or describe decoration. A complex spatial figure
also receives a long description that expresses every node, edge, direction,
branch, gate, label, and limitation in reading order. Numeric values and gate
conditions remain in adjacent semantic text even when a figure illustrates
them. Decorative elements receive no misleading technical description.

The cover is a semantic composition: Warm Bench Paper, a Bench Black
instrument window, exactly three separately labeled evidence traces, a visible
Qualification Gate, bench plate, exact title/subtitle, and Komal Nakrani as live
text. Reject neural glow, robots, stock laboratories, fake interfaces, generic
mascots, logos, watermarks, generated text, or any inherited identity.

## Edition and errata route

Each edition records its identifier, date, parent edition, affected
claims/sources/cases/figures, source rechecks, accessibility review, and
disposition. Publication status, ISBN, print date, and public URLs belong to
that record and must not be inferred. Errata preserve the original statement,
correction, impact, owner, and effective edition.

Accessibility defects are evidence defects. A missing header, ambiguous link,
color-only state, unreadable contrast, absent long description, rasterized
numeric truth, or broken keyboard/PDF reading order blocks publication. About
Komal and the closing statement remain selectable live text in
[the opening and closing record](opening-and-closing.md); a portrait is not
required for the author's identity or for any technical meaning.
