## Freeze procedure

At manuscript, visual, and publication freeze, resolve the canonical source,
record access date and version, compare material wording, and decide whether the
claim remains supported inside the existing limit. If a trigger fires, do not
quietly swap a URL. Preserve the old identity, add or replace evidence through
the review chain, update every affected claim/case/currentness note, and rerun
reverse-edge validation. A living documentation mechanism may change while the
durable engineering doctrine remains; keep those two statements separate.
