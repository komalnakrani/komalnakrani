## FURN-BZ-05 — MLE ownership boundary

The MLE owns the workload-facing engineering decision: make the approved task
executable; bind the relevant identities; construct reproducible-enough tests;
assemble qualification, release, observation, recovery, control, and retirement
evidence; and route each unresolved decision to its actual owner. The MLE may
issue a technical `PASS`, `HOLD`, or `REJECT` within the bounded workload
contract. The MLE may not turn that disposition into product authorization,
domain validity, independent evaluation, fleet capacity, incident command,
security or safety acceptance, privacy or legal permission, governance policy,
or business approval.

| Decision | MLE contribution | Authority that remains external |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose, intended use, and priority | Translate an approved purpose into testable artifacts and exclusions | Product and domain owners |
| Population, labels, and consequential error | Implement contracts, measurements, and diagnostics | Domain, data, evaluation, and relevant formal owners |
| Scientific novelty and method validity | Preserve run and comparison integrity | Applied Science and independent evaluation |
| Shared runtime and fleet action | Measure the workload envelope and supply diagnostics | Platform, SRE, and incident command |
| Controls and exceptions | Implement and test workload controls | Security, safety, privacy, governance, legal, and risk owners |
| Release or retirement | Assemble resolvable evidence and verify technical state | Product, platform, domain, and formal approvers |

A missing owner is a failed gate, not permission by default. The escalation
record names the decision, present evidence, unresolved consequence, actual
owner, requested action, response deadline when applicable, and next evidence.
No companion command grants authority.

## FURN-BZ-06 — Lifecycle and evidence legend

The state rail is a chain of reviewable propositions, not an automated
promotion pipeline:

`UNORIENTED → ORIENTED → CONTRACTED → ADMISSIBLE → RECONSTRUCTIBLE → CANDIDATE → TECHNICALLY-QUALIFIED → RELEASABLE → OPERABLE → OBSERVED → REQUALIFIED or ROLLED-BACK → CONTROLLED → RETIRED → REVIEWED`

Some chapters preserve the same state while adding required evidence. At the
qualification boundary, `CANDIDATE` may become `TECHNICALLY-QUALIFIED`, `HOLD`,
or `REJECT`. `HOLD → RELEASABLE` and `REJECT → RELEASABLE` are forbidden: the
missing or failed evidence must be repaired and reviewed through a legal route.

Evidence maturity also needs words. A **model file** is bytes. A **tracked
run** adds bounded execution identity. A **candidate** adds controlled
comparison evidence. **Technically qualified** records a complete bounded
disposition. **Releasable** binds executable, consumers, integrity, approvals,
and recovery identity. **Operable** adds a measured serving envelope.
**Observed** adds owned signals without inventing ground truth. **Reviewed**
means the complete retired dossier survived final examination; it does not
erase remaining limits.

- `PASS` means the named gate's required evidence is present and the decision
  is supported inside its stated boundary.
- `HOLD` preserves the candidate and evidence while a named gap, owner, or
  dependency remains unresolved.
- `REJECT` records that the current proposition failed; it does not authorize
  deleting the failed evidence.
- `REOPEN` invalidates downstream confidence when a material premise changes.

Four reopen families route changed purpose or intended use to `CONTRACTED`;
changed data, labels, features, or population to `ADMISSIBLE`; changed runtime,
dependencies, interface, or serving envelope to `RECONSTRUCTIBLE`; and changed
authority, constraint, or permitted use to `CONTRACTED`. Reopening creates new
evidence and preserves the old record.

## FURN-BZ-07 — How to read a Bench Sheet

Every chapter uses six recurring elements.

1. **Bench Setup** names the decision job, incoming state, prerequisite
   artifacts, and the failure pressure that makes the chapter necessary.
2. **Bench Sheet** turns the chapter's three canonical claims into checks,
   expected evidence, counterexamples, limitations, and an external authority
   route.
3. **Qualification Gate** returns `PASS`, `HOLD`, `REJECT`, or `REOPEN` for the
   stated question. It never promotes merely because a command completed.
4. **State and dossier delta** states what changed and writes exactly one new
   `BL-00` through `BL-20` record without rewriting prior dossier bytes.
5. **Authority route** identifies the owner of every decision the MLE cannot
   make.
6. **Next evidence** names the exact artifact the next chapter is allowed to
   consume.

Read tables by header and row label, not by color. Read a synthetic lab as a
counterexample-capable demonstration of mechanics. Its fixed values can prove
that a validator detected a mutation; they cannot prove a real system is safe,
fast, fair, reliable, permitted, or valuable. Each public case separates
reported facts, attributed outcomes, allowed transfer, forbidden inference,
and limitations. Each companion command must target a fresh directory outside
the repository and emits a new canonical record; that output is not a release
approval.

## FURN-BZ-09 — Project and case orientation

`CASE-01`, the Benchline Inspection Dossier, is a fictional synthetic capstone.
It carries a constructed edge inspection workload through the entire book.
Every request, image, label, threshold, owner action, result, and incident is a
fixture. It supports practice with evidence shape and failure injection; it
supports no real industrial, safety, performance, or business claim.

`CASE-02` through `CASE-05` are constructed satellites: a managed demand
forecast, classical credit triage, deep acoustic event classifier, and shared
ranking-platform tenant. They test transfer across mechanisms. They are not
cloud guidance, financial advice or credit authorization, a deep-learning
recipe, or a platform-engineering curriculum.

`CASE-06` through `CASE-12` are public reported cases. They cover the Google ML
Test Score method, Uber Michelangelo reports, PyTorch reproducibility guidance,
Gender Shades, ONNX versioning, the PyTorch nightly dependency incident, and the
Knight Capital deployment/control failure. Reported facts remain attributed and
dated. Outcomes are never generalized beyond the source. See
[Appendix E](appendix-e.md) before transferring any lesson.

The five ports—managed, classical, deep, edge, and shared platform—change
mechanics, not the decision envelope. Each must preserve the same disposition
shape, evidence identifiers, limitations, external authority, and dossier
lineage. A provider dashboard, local library, accelerator, device runtime, or
shared service may supply mechanisms; none qualifies the workload by itself.

## FURN-CLOSE-01 — Completed dossier index

The completed reader dossier contains `BL-00` through `BL-20` in hash-linked
order. `BL-00` orients the workload; `BL-01` contracts task and authority;
`BL-02` retains the incumbent and consequences; `BL-03` and `BL-04` bind data,
labels, features, transformations, feedback, and lineage; `BL-05` establishes
admissibility; `BL-06` through `BL-08` preserve controlled comparison, bounded
run identity, and candidate nomination. `BL-09` and `BL-10` preserve population,
segment, uncertainty, and failure evidence; `BL-11` records the explicit
qualification outcome. `BL-12` through `BL-14` bind the package, consumer
contract, integrity, and recovery identity. `BL-15` through `BL-17` preserve
the serving envelope, observation, containment, rollback, changed identity, and
requalification route. `BL-18` closes control and exception routing; `BL-19`
records retirement and bounded non-serving evidence; `BL-20` records the final
review and reusable-standard proposal.

Audit the chain by resolving every prior hash, artifact ID, version, state,
disposition, limitation, authority route, and next-evidence link. A missing
record, unresolved link, illegal transition, silent repair, or self-approval is
a failed final review.

## FURN-CLOSE-02 — Workload standard card

The reusable standard is compact: contract the purpose and owners; retain the
incumbent; bind every material input and transformation; compare under a
declared design; preserve bounded run identity; qualify by population,
consequence, and limitation; release exact bytes plus interfaces, consumers,
approvals, and recovery identity; measure the serving envelope; observe without
inventing truth; change through containment and requalification; retain formal
decisions; retire known paths; then review the whole dossier.

Minimum evidence is the evidence required by the current gate, not every
artifact an organization could collect. Owner routes remain explicit.
Reopen when purpose or intended use, data or population, runtime or interface,
or authority or permitted use changes. Preserve the superseded record so that
the next reviewer can see both the prior decision and the new premise.

## FURN-CLOSE-03 — Systemic escalation record

A workload review may reveal a shared-platform defect, missing policy,
insufficient evaluation service, unsafe default, or repeated manual control.
Record it without absorbing ownership:

| Field | Required entry |
| --- | --- |
| Finding | Observable cross-workload condition and affected evidence |
| Classification | Platform issue, policy issue, or unresolved boundary |
| Actual owner | Named team or authority able to decide and act |
| Workload containment | Local action already taken without claiming systemic resolution |
| Requested decision | Specific question the owner must answer |
| Next evidence | Artifact that would support closure or reopening |

Routing a finding is advanced MLE practice. Declaring fleet reliability,
organization policy, or formal acceptance is not.

## FURN-CLOSE-04 — Final review and next practice

The terminal state is `REVIEWED` only after the workload is `RETIRED` and the
complete dossier has survived a hostile review. Remaining limits stay visible:
inventory and observation-time evidence cannot prove the absence of every
unknown path; synthetic labs cannot prove production behavior; public cases do
not predict local outcomes; and a technically complete record cannot replace an
external authorization.

For next practice, replay one chapter with a deliberately corrupted fixture,
port the same decision across all five mechanisms, or review a real workload's
artifacts with its owners. Begin from its approved purpose and current
authority map. Do not reuse the fictional results, copy a `PASS`, or treat this
book's final state as automatic release permission.
