## Misuse prohibitions

- Do not change data, preprocessing, metric definitions, or threshold semantics and still call a result incumbent-relative improvement.
- Do not treat aggregate metric gain as evidence that every relevant segment or consequence is acceptable.
- Do not let the MLE self-approve disputed tolerance or independent gate adequacy.
- Do not use the ML Test Score as a universal point threshold or certification.
- Do not present constructed credit-triage decisions as financial advice, domain authorization, or a real outcome.
- Do not freeze current libraries or algorithms into durable doctrine.
