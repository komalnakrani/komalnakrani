## Exact book claims

1. `MLE-BCLM-004` (`technical-doctrine`, high confidence, durable): Before model qualification, the task contract must record intended and excluded uses, users or population, operating context, expected impacts, assumptions, limitations, and the evidence needed to judge the stated purpose.
2. `MLE-BCLM-005` (`technical-doctrine`, high confidence, durable): Decision rights, risk responsibilities, and lines of communication must be documented; when a required authority is absent or the intended purpose cannot be evaluated, the technically honest disposition is HOLD rather than silent self-approval.
3. `MLE-BCLM-006` (`technical-method`, medium confidence, contextual): A task contract should preserve a NO-ML branch by measuring a simple heuristic or incumbent first and requiring a stated reason that learning is a justified intervention rather than an assumed destination.
