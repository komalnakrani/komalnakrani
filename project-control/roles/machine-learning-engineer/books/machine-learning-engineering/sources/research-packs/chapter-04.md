## Exact book claims

1. `MLE-BCLM-010` (`technical-doctrine`, high confidence, durable): A dataset and label contract should carry origin, motivation, composition, collection and annotation processes, intended and excluded uses, transformations, known limitations, maintenance, and responsible contacts from creator to consumer.
2. `MLE-BCLM-011` (`technical-mechanism`, high confidence, contextual): Executable schemas, descriptive statistics, and anomaly comparisons can fail malformed or shifted inputs, but a clean schema cannot establish label meaning, provenance, permission, representativeness, or fitness for the intended decision.
3. `MLE-BCLM-012` (`technical-doctrine`, high confidence, durable): Data quality, label validity, access, retention, and population exceptions cross organizational and formal boundaries; the MLE owns workload-facing conformance and escalation evidence, while data, domain, privacy, legal, and governance owners retain their decisions.

## Durable, volatile, and conflicting evidence treatment

- Keep dataset/label identity, provenance, meaning, population, permissions, limitations, maintenance, contacts, and exception routing as durable fields.
- Keep executable schema and anomaly mechanisms contextual. `MLE-BSRC-024` is living documentation and must be rechecked at every freeze or API/threshold semantic change.
- Version every dataset snapshot, label definition, transformation, permission state, retention input, responsible owner, threshold, and exception.
- Datasheets/Data Cards and executable validation are complementary: narrative provenance cannot replace conformance checks, while passing conformance cannot prove provenance or validity.
- Sector-specific representativeness guidance and empirical cascade evidence bound examples; neither establishes a universal rule or frequency.

## Five-port transfer

- `PORT-MANAGED`: require exportable snapshot, schema, label, provenance, permission, owner, and exception evidence.
- `PORT-CLASSICAL`: apply identical contract depth even when data and model are tabular or simple.
- `PORT-DEEP`: bind annotations, preprocessing, and checkpoint inputs to exact identities.
- `PORT-EDGE`: preserve sensor, device, field, and delayed-label context with the dataset identity.
- `PORT-SHARED`: separate tenant dataset/label evidence from shared ingestion and storage authority.

## Misuse prohibitions

- Do not claim that a datasheet, data card, or clean schema proves truth, representativeness, permission, or fitness.
- Do not treat a validation library threshold as a domain or formal decision.
- Do not generalize medical-device guidance into legal or non-medical authorization.
- Do not present the Data Cascades study as universal prevalence or causal magnitude.
- Do not let the MLE approve access, retention, privacy, legal basis, governance, or domain label validity.
- Do not import source prose, figures, or vendor-specific UI into planned artifacts.
