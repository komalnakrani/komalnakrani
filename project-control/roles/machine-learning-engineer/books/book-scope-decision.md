# Machine Learning Engineer — Book Scope and Volume Decision

## Decision Record

Decision: `SINGLE BOOK`

Publish one original Komal professional book that carries one versioned learning
workload from an uncertain request through task and data contracts, bounded
experiments, technical qualification, release identity, measured operation,
incident response, requalification, and evidence-preserving retirement.

The working publication identity for Phase 05 is:

> **Machine Learning Engineering**
> *From Task Contract to Operating Evidence*

Author: **Komal Nakrani**.

The title remains subject to Phase 05 architecture QA. The one-book boundary is
frozen here because the accepted evidence forms one revisable dependency graph.
Splitting that graph would repeat the workload identity, authority map,
qualification evidence, and lifecycle dossier without producing a second
independent profession or reader endpoint.

## Minimum Structure

The minimum coherent structure is one substantial lifecycle book organized
around the eight accepted learning clusters `LC-01` through `LC-08`. Phase 05
may group those clusters into parts and chapters, but it may not remove a
durable decision, detach cross-functional judgment into a generic leadership
unit, or create a second volume to absorb repeated evidence.

The book must contain one continuous capstone dossier, bounded satellite cases
across all five required contexts, deterministic companion artifacts, labs with
truthful evidence states, learning checks, source-linked claims, and a complete
screen-first edition. It must remain usable both as a guided progression and as
a later professional reference.

## Thesis and Reader Endpoints

Machine learning engineering is the discipline of turning an approved purpose
and admissible data into a versioned learning workload whose candidate,
qualification evidence, executable identity, operating envelope, recovery
path, and retirement record remain inspectable as the system changes.

The entry reader can train or integrate models but may still confuse a tracked
run, metric, registry entry, deployment, or dashboard with a justified
technical disposition. The completing reader can construct, qualify, release,
observe, diagnose, requalify, and retire a bounded learning workload while
keeping product, domain, evaluation, platform, SRE, security, safety, privacy,
governance, legal, and risk-acceptance authority explicit and external.

## Audience and Prerequisites

Primary readers are software, data, applied-AI, and early-to-mid-career ML
engineers moving into end-to-end workload accountability, plus technical leads
who must review that evidence. Readers should know production programming,
version control, tests, APIs, basic statistics, supervised-learning vocabulary,
common model-evaluation concepts, and ordinary service/deployment concepts.

No specific cloud, framework, registry, orchestrator, accelerator, or model
family is a prerequisite. The book must preserve comparable reasoning across
managed services, classical/statistical systems, deep-learning systems,
physical/edge workloads, and shared-platform environments.

## Exclusions and Dependencies

The book does not teach beginner programming, generalized data engineering,
novel-model research, LLM post-training specialization, agent execution,
complete AI-product behavior engineering, customer-engagement ownership,
generalized MLOps/platform/SRE infrastructure, red teaming, security or safety
assurance, privacy/legal compliance, product prioritization, or domain risk
acceptance. It consumes those partners' contracts and services and returns
versioned workload evidence without taking their authority.

Its dependency order is revisable rather than one-way: purpose and authority
shape admissible data; data and baselines shape experiments; experiments feed
representative qualification; qualification binds a release package; live
evidence may trigger containment, rollback, repair, retraining, requalification,
or retirement; any change may reopen an upstream contract.

## Capstone Model

The capstone is the fictional **Benchline Inspection Dossier**. A manufacturing
team proposes automated defect triage for a constrained inspection line. The
reader must preserve a rule-based incumbent, define the technical task and
decision owners, establish admissible sensor/image and label contracts, compare
classical and deep candidates, qualify segments and uncertainty, package an
exact edge-compatible workload, stage release, diagnose a shifted operating
condition, choose containment or repair, requalify a change, and close with a
non-serving retirement record.

Every result is local, synthetic, illustrative, or explicitly recorded as such.
The dossier never claims production performance, business approval, domain
acceptance, safety assurance, or legal authorization. Satellite bench ports
test the same reasoning in managed, classical/statistical, deep-learning,
physical/edge, and shared-platform contexts so the capstone does not become a
model-family curriculum.

## Approximate Depth

Phase 05 should target roughly 18–22 chapters in five to seven parts, about
140,000–170,000 original manuscript words excluding registries, companion code,
and visual metadata. This range is a depth forecast, not a page quota. A longer
screen-first PDF is acceptable when typography, diagrams, labs, or evidence
records become easier to read; any page or element that does not improve a
decision, comprehension, accessibility, or traceability must be removed.

Each cluster requires conceptual, practical, architecture, implementation,
operational, security/authority, project, prerequisite, and narrative depth.
Advanced depth widens ambiguity, consequence, architectural radius, initiative,
mentoring, and evidence standards rather than adding fashionable algorithms.

## Visual Production Implications

The book uses the approved **Learning Systems Test Bench** system: Warm Bench
Paper, Bench Black, Instrument Blue, Calibration Lime, Diagnostic Rose, Grid
Steel, Archivo SemiCondensed, Source Sans 3, and IBM Plex Mono in a 7 by 10 inch
screen-first edition. Bench Zero, Bench Setup, Bench Sheet, Qualification Gate,
and the closing dossier must explain evidence and decision movement rather than
decorate pages.

Exact plots, axes, thresholds, identifiers, tables, and overlays use semantic
HTML/CSS so text stays selectable and precise. Original ImageGen PNGs are
commissioned only for explanatory dimensional concepts; Astro controls delivery
optimization, and no SVG or WebP publication figure is stored. No generic
mascot is used. Komal's identity may appear only when a specific pedagogical
role justifies it and the approved local identity references are preserved.
Every retained figure requires purpose, facts, short verified labels, caption,
alt text, long description where needed, prompt, avoid list, dimensions,
canonical path, and SHA-256 provenance.

## Alternatives Considered

- `SINGLE BOOK` — **retained**. One complete professional endpoint and one
  accumulating dossier preserve the dependency graph without repeated seams.
- `2-VOLUME SERIES` — **rejected for the canonical structure**. It is the only
  credible fallback, but its qualification/operation seam requires an exact
  frozen dossier and duplicates orientation, authority, and evidence context.
  The same content is more coherent and usable in one navigable book.
- `3-VOLUME SERIES` — **rejected and merged**. Evaluation, release, and
  requalification evidence would be taught on both sides of two seams, while
  `LC-08` would be repeatedly reintroduced.
- `4+-VOLUME SERIES` — **rejected and merged**. Contract/data,
  experiment/evaluation, release/operation, and control/retirement cannot become
  independent endpoints without deferring essential controls and replaying the
  complete workload dossier.

Framework, cloud, model family, fashionable keyword, inherited publication
size, seniority label, and one-cluster-per-book symmetry are invalid split bases.
