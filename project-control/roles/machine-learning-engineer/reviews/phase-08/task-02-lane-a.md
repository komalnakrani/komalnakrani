## Blocking findings

### P08-LA-001 — Total word compliance hides a severe departure from the frozen section plan

Prose-only section accounting against the Blueprint Register found only 9 of 56
sections inside their frozen depth bands; 47 are outside. S02 is overconcentrated
in every chapter: 1,064, 1,376, 1,219, 1,402, 1,577, 1,682, and 1,728 words

450–550; S05 is below its 500–600 range in six chapters; and the assessment/gate
sections in Chapters 04–07 are only 183, 174, 150, and 169 words against
450–550. Several evidence-interpretation and handoff sections are also short,
while some worked traces exceed their bands.

The prose is relevant and not exact-line filler, but the allocation leaves setup,
diagnostic reasoning, assessment, and durable handoff at summary depth while the
procedure becomes a second mini-chapter. This does not implement the frozen
eight-section production sequence as a balanced full-length teaching chapter.

Required repair: rewrite and redistribute all seven chapters so every S01–S08
section carries its frozen production job and lands within its planned depth
band. Preserve the total chapter ranges with chapter-specific method,
counterexample, lab diagnosis, observable answer reasoning, and handoff prose;
do not replace the imbalance with padding or repeated boilerplate.
