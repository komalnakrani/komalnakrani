# Chapter 20 Blueprint — Earn Reuse From Repeated Evidence

## Purpose and exit capability

Decide which evaluation, adapter, context, policy/control, observability, or model mechanisms have earned reuse and which product evidence, thresholds, segments, and authority remain local.

## Prerequisites and non-scope

- Prerequisite: full Patchwork dossier and model-change replay.
- Non-scope: generalized platform roadmap, universal model, organizational architecture mandate, or reuse from one success.

## Concepts, skills, and decision models

- Reuse ladder: local fix -> repeated pattern -> configurable component -> shared service -> platform candidate.
- Stable seam, compatible tasks, local assumptions, owners/SLO, isolation, migration, adoption/maintenance economics.
- Pattern ledger and disconfirmation; reusable mechanism versus evidence form versus decision.
- Skill: explicitly choose keep local, copy, reuse, standardize, retire, or test again.

## Architecture, implementation, and stability

- Companion: extract stable schemas/eval runner/provider adapter/redaction utilities; keep compatibility policy/data/thresholds local; run cross-case validation.
- Reuse tests are `durable`; registries/framework platforms are `replaceable`.

## Scenario and artifact progression

Extend `PF-12` to v0.2 with pattern ledger, reuse proposal, consumer/owner contract, migration and disconfirmation plan. Reject at least one tempting abstraction.

## Cases and bounded use

- `AAE-C004`: canonical ranker benefits and coupling.
- `AAE-C007`: centralized propagation of data assumptions.

## Failures, disputes, and tradeoffs

One feature as platform; copy always bad; adapter as portability; shared threshold/control across consequences; no consumer migration plan. Leverage, local quality, coordination, blast radius, and maintenance conflict.

## Exercise, companion work, and completion evidence

Classify 10 assets, extract one shared mechanism, validate against a satellite case, reject one abstraction, and write disconfirmation. Pass when assumptions, consumers, owner, version/change, fallback, and local evidence are explicit.
