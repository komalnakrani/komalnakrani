# Chapter 21 Blueprint — Lead Applied AI Decisions

## Purpose and exit capability

Set evidence standards, allocate technical attention across systems, communicate uncertainty at several altitudes, mentor teams, create shared practice, and escalate decisions outside technical authority.

## Prerequisites and non-scope

- Prerequisite: complete Patchwork evidence/change/reuse dossier.
- Non-scope: generic people management, product roadmap ownership, commercial strategy, compensation/career ladder, or legal/safety/privacy/security/risk authority.

## Concepts, skills, and decision models

- Portfolio evidence map: consequence, evidence gap, active change, operational burden, control, deadline, leverage.
- Decision altitudes: implementation, system, product, portfolio, specialist, formal authority.
- Evidence standard, escalation brief, delegation/mentoring, technical strategy, stop/decline decision.
- Skill: preserve uncertainty while making a concrete recommendation and naming its owner.

## Architecture, implementation, and stability

- Architecture content: portfolio and governance information flows; no new product mechanism.
- Companion: final dossier verifier and three-system portfolio scenario; all outputs are local reports.
- Leadership decision models are `durable`; employer titles/levels/team structures are `contextual/volatile`.

## Scenario and artifact progression

Complete `PF-12` v1.0 with portfolio review, evidence-standard memo, escalation brief, operating cadence, and growth plan. Patchwork is compared with high-consequence classification and bounded tool-assistant satellites.

## Cases and bounded use

- `AAE-C001`: qualitative signal versus launch pressure.
- `AAE-C004`: reuse versus shared blast radius.
- `AAE-C007`: organizational data failure conditions.
- `AAE-C012`: constructed portfolio and career evidence only.

## Failures, disputes, and tradeoffs

Leadership as heroics; model count as impact; generic maturity model; portfolio by revenue/hype alone; influence as authority; firefighting as excellence. Speed, consequence, evidence maturity, staff attention, and leverage conflict.

## Exercise, companion work, and completion evidence

Allocate attention across three systems, defend stop/escalate/delegate/reuse decisions, and run the final dossier verifier. Pass when every recommendation names evidence, uncertainty, owner, authority, next gate, and declined responsibility.
