# Chapter 01 Blueprint — Behavior, Not Magic

## Purpose and exit capability

Establish the Applied AI Engineer's unit of accountability and the complete evidence loop. The reader can distinguish capability, model component, product behavior, and authorized outcome; classify adjacent-role ownership; and write a responsibility charter that makes technical evidence and formal authority separate.

## Prerequisites and non-scope

- Prerequisite: production application literacy from the architecture entry profile.
- Non-scope: universal employer definitions, model science, product-roadmap ownership, customer-engagement ownership, platform ownership, or formal risk acceptance.

## Concepts, skills, and decision models

- Capability/component/system/outcome stack; behavior as the observable contract surface.
- Evidence loop: task -> contract -> mechanism/system -> evaluation -> release/operation -> feedback/change.
- Responsibility classifier: primary decision, artifact, evidence, consequence, owner, authority, escalation.
- Skill: diagnose whether a polished AI demo is missing product, evaluation, operational, or authority evidence.

## Architecture, implementation, and stability

- Architecture content: system/lifecycle boundary only; component topology begins in Chapter 7.
- Durable format: Markdown responsibility charter and evidence-loop record.
- Current role/vendor examples are `volatile/contextual`; do not make a job posting or model specification the role standard.

## Scenario and artifact progression

Patchwork movement: replace “add an AI finder” with a bounded hypothesis about evidence-backed repair-part discovery. Produce `PF-01` responsibility charter, boundary memo, and evidence-loop map v0.1.

## Failures, disputes, and tradeoffs

Model equals system; demo equals readiness; “end to end” equals unlimited ownership; title equals authority. Broad behavior ownership improves continuity but creates overreach unless platform, product, domain, and risk decisions are explicit.

## Exercise, companion work, and completion evidence
