## Mechanism and satellite coverage

- Mandatory non-LLM path: deterministic incompatibility rules plus multimodal/hybrid retrieval and ranking remain a complete product path.
- Predictive transfer: classification/ranking/forecasting scenarios use the same task-contract-evaluation-release loop without chat or retrieval where inappropriate.
- Generative path: explanation generation must improve comprehension without weakening evidence fidelity, segment quality, latency, or controls.
- Bounded tool path: seller-question drafting cannot execute contact or purchase and requires permission, validation, confirmation, audit, and recovery.
- High-consequence transfer: clinical/care examples teach segment evidence, professional review, escalation, and authority limits without teaching medicine.
- Reuse transfer: shared mechanisms must pass a second context and include a disconfirmation path.
