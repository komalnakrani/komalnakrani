# Applied AI Engineering — ImageGen Production Log

## Production policy

- Tool: built-in ImageGen.
- Final format: original generated PNG; no SVG or WebP derivative is canonical.
- Intended use: full-width explanatory technical-book figures for web and print.
- Shared style prompt: premium realistic 3D editorial infographic; warm ivory studio background; tactile glass, ceramic, and metal; coral, teal, indigo, sunflower yellow, and mint; colorful, crisp, approachable, professional; landscape composition; labels legible at book width.
- Shared constraints: one dominant teaching idea; short essential labels rendered verbatim; no logos, vendor marks, or watermark; no decorative AI robot; no generic presenter or mascot. A future Komal mascot may appear only when pedagogically necessary and must use the original-photo identity references in `/Applications/ServBay/www/komal/mascot/`.
- Review: every accepted image was inspected in the generation result for subject, hierarchy, text, color, and the requested boundary/sequence/state relationship.
