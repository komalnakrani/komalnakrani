# Chapter 21 Research — Lead Applied AI Decisions

## Architecture anchors

- Primary domains: `AAE-K01`, `AAE-K08`, `AAE-K10`, `AAE-K11`
- Dossier milestone: `PF-12`
- Forecast figures: `F21.1`, `F21.2`

## Research question

How should an advanced Applied AI Engineer set evidence standards, allocate attention across systems, communicate uncertainty, create shared practice, and influence decisions without absorbing product or formal-risk authority?

## Claim and evidence map

- **C21.1 — AI governance requires explicit roles, communication, and continuous lifecycle practice.** `AAE-S001` and `AAE-S054` support governance and cross-functional risk activity.
- **C21.2 — Applied-AI leadership must manage cross-component and cross-team engineering, not model work in isolation.** `AAE-S005`, `AAE-S006`, and `AAE-S013` support collaboration, technical debt, and organizational data dependencies.
- **C21.3 — Shared evidence systems can organize claims and limitations without replacing review.** `AAE-S055` supports system factsheets; `AAE-S011` and `AAE-S012` add data/model documentation boundaries.
- **C21.4 — Security frameworks support coordinated control practice while formal ownership remains organizational.** `AAE-S037`, `AAE-S038`, and `AAE-S042` provide security/privacy/control frames.
- **C21.5 — A severe behavior miss may require changing evaluation taxonomy and decision process, not only the model.** `AAE-S046` provides current first-party evidence.
- **C21.6 — Reuse decisions require maintenance, quality, and blast-radius tradeoffs across consumers.** `AAE-S022` supplies a concrete first-party case.

## Leadership evidence packet

Portfolio inventory, user consequence, evidence maturity, active change exposure, operational burden, control gaps, ownership/authority, reusable opportunity, decision deadline, disconfirming evidence, escalation, and communication at implementation/system/product/portfolio altitudes.

## Cases

- `AAE-C001` asks how leaders respond when qualitative evidence conflicts with positive launch signals.
- `AAE-C004` asks whether reuse improves leverage or centralizes risk.
- `AAE-C007` exposes organizational conditions that make data failures recur.
- `AAE-C012` closes with a multi-system portfolio review, evidence-standard memo, and explicit escalation boundaries.

## Disputes and limits

There is no universal maturity ladder or team shape. Seniority should be demonstrated by decisions, evidence, systems improved, and others enabled—not model count, headcount, or title. Technical leaders can recommend priorities but product/business owners decide roadmap; they can produce risk evidence but accountable authorities authorize.

## Remaining gaps

No release-blocking gap. Career levels and compensation are employer-specific and deliberately excluded from durable claims.

## Blueprint constraints

Require allocation across three systems with conflicting consequence, evidence gap, deadline, operational load, and leverage. The correct response must include a decision to escalate or decline authority.

## Manuscript prohibitions

Do not turn leadership into generic management advice, glorify firefighting, or imply influence grants product, legal, safety, privacy, security, or risk-acceptance authority.
