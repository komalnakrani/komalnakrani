# Phase 04 — Applied AI Engineer Book Scope and Volume Decision

## Objective

Determine the smallest coherent set of original Komal books needed to teach Applied AI Engineering without artificial splits, duplicated lifecycle material, or an LLM/agent-only definition of the role.

## Inputs

- Phase 01 role validation and exact verdict `PROCEED`
- structured register of 15 primary sources and 17 material claims
- adjacent-role boundary and explicit non-scope
- locked Komal-first execution order
- competency standard and objective map: intentionally deferred to Abhyaas and treated as a later compatibility check, not a blocker to Komal scope
- existing Applied AI Engineer manuscripts: none; all content will be researched and written fresh

## Required comparison

Evaluate at least these alternatives before deciding:

1. one lifecycle book from task selection through monitored operation;
2. two books separating behavior construction from production operation and leadership;
3. a larger multi-volume reference split by data/context, evaluation, runtime operations, or AI mechanism.

For every option, test whether each proposed volume has an independently substantial thesis, reader transformation, project arc, and non-duplicated ownership boundary.

## Durable learning clusters to size

1. task, user value, and the decision to use AI;
2. behavior contracts and system boundaries;
3. model, mechanism, and interface selection;
4. data, context, retrieval, and grounding;
5. production application and integration engineering;
6. evaluation, experimentation, and evidence;
7. reliability, latency, cost, and observability;
8. responsible controls, authority, safety, and governance;
9. release, monitoring, feedback, incident response, and iteration;
10. model/provider change, migration, reuse, and portability;
11. advanced technical leadership and organizational practice.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every durable learning cluster has an explicit depth and dependency estimate.
- [x] One-book, two-book, and larger multi-volume alternatives are compared honestly.
- [x] The decision uses learning/reference utility, not inherited FDE architecture, product count, or fashionable tooling.
- [x] Every retained volume has a distinct one-sentence thesis and reader transformation.
- [x] Domain ownership, dependencies, non-overlap rules, exclusions, and deferred material are explicit.
- [x] The chosen structure covers non-agentic, multimodal, predictive, retrieval, recommendation, and generative cases at the correct abstraction.
- [x] `books/book-scope-decision.md` is complete and directly actionable for Phase 05.

## Verification

- Re-run the merge test: if two proposed volumes cannot sustain distinct theses without repeating task formulation, evaluation, release, and feedback boundaries, merge them.
- Check the scope against every adjacent-role boundary from Phase 01.
- Confirm no inherited Alpesh, FDE, MLOps, or Abhyaas content or structure has entered the decision.
- Run publication validation after the state transition.

## Output

- `project-control/roles/applied-ai-engineer/books/book-scope-decision.md`
- updated `project-control/roles/applied-ai-engineer/ROLE-STATE.md`

Phase 05 may design the complete book or series architecture without reconsidering volume count unless new evidence contradicts a documented assumption.
