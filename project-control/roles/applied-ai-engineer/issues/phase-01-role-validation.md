# Phase 01 - Validate Applied AI Engineer Role and Boundary

GitHub issue: [#22](https://github.com/alpeshznakrani/komalnakrani/issues/22)

## Objective

Prove whether Applied AI Engineer is a credible standalone professional role and define a precise, non-overlapping boundary suitable for original Komal book architecture.

## Required research

- Canonical title, durable aliases, and current employer usage across multiple organizations and industries.
- Daily, strategic, architecture, implementation, operational, security/governance, stakeholder, and advanced responsibilities.
- Durable tools/technology categories without turning a volatile stack into the role definition.
- Outputs, deliverables, common failures, seniority shape, and career adjacency.
- Explicit comparison with Forward Deployed Engineer, Agentic AI Engineer, LLM Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, AI Research Engineer, AI Solutions Architect, software engineering, data engineering, MLOps/platform engineering, AI product management, and AI safety/security specialists.

## Evidence policy

- Prefer current official employer/career pages, official documentation, standards organizations, primary research, and official engineering publications.
- Use no job aggregators or anonymous role summaries for material role claims when primary evidence exists.
- Preserve title volatility, employer-specific breadth, and inference limits in every source record.
- Do not import the completed FDE boundary, book architecture, cases, or content as an Applied AI substitute.

## Verdict

Issue exactly one: `PROCEED`, `RENAME TO <name>`, `MERGE WITH <role>`, `KEEP AS SPECIALIZATION`, or `REJECT`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Current official evidence supports or rejects the canonical title explicitly.
- [x] Responsibilities cover implementation, architecture, evaluation, operations, security/governance, stakeholders, and deliverables.
- [x] Evidence spans multiple employers and industries rather than one company vocabulary.
- [x] Adjacent-role overlap and explicit non-scope are resolved at decision/accountability depth.
- [x] Every material claim maps to a structured source record.
- [x] Research limitations and unstable/tool-specific details are explicit.
- [x] The verdict is exact, supported, and actionable for Komal book-scope work.

## Handoff

If and only if the verdict is `PROCEED`, close Phase 01 and open Komal Phase 04 book-scope work. Do not enter deferred Abhyaas phases.
