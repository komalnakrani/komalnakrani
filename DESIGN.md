# Design System: Komal Screen-First Publications

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Field Expedition Log"**

The publication should feel like a reader is moving through a consequential field assignment rather than consuming an exported manuscript. Orientation pages establish the territory, chapter openings mark a new coordinate, body pages make evidence easy to scan, and decision checkpoints interrupt at the exact moment judgment is required. The tone is rigorous but never austere: color, diagrams, and spatial variety serve comprehension and stamina.

The first expression is *Forward Deployed Engineering*. Its visual world joins field logs, operational maps, annotated evidence, checkpoint stamps, and route markers. The system must extend coherently across the cover, title and copyright pages, contents, part dividers, chapter openings, body pages, figures, code, tables, exercises, appendices, sources, author page, and closing page.

Each future book receives a genre-specific palette and motif. Shared Komal identity comes from clarity, hierarchy, explanatory visual elements, generous screen-reading rhythm, and complete authorship and edition design - not from forcing one palette onto every title.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Screen-first reading with larger type, shorter line length, and generous pacing.
- Strong alternation between dramatic orientation pages and calm reading pages.
- Field coordinates, route lines, evidence markers, and checkpoint stamps as functional wayfinding.
- Original explanatory imagery; no decorative filler or copied reference-book assets.
- Full-book design from opening cover through closing page.

## Colors

The Forward Deployed Engineering palette is a committed four-role system: midnight field blue for orientation and depth, expedition cyan for evidence and maps, signal orange for decisions and interventions, and cool paper for sustained reading.

### Primary

- **Midnight Field Blue** (`#07192F`): part openers, cover fields, navigation bands, and high-level orientation.

### Secondary

- **Expedition Cyan** (`#00A8CF`): evidence paths, diagrams, explanatory highlights, and active coordinates.

### Tertiary

- **Signal Orange** (`#FF7A1A`): decisions, warnings, handoffs, checkpoints, and sparse focal accents.

### Neutral

- **Cool Reading Paper** (`#F4F7FB`): primary long-form reading surface.
- **Deep Ink** (`#10233C`): body text and structural rules on light pages.
- **Mist Blue** (`#D9F7FF`): evidence callout fields and low-intensity diagram regions.

**The Signal Means Decision Rule.** Orange is never general decoration. It identifies a decision, intervention, exception, warning, or committed handoff.

**The Genre Owns Its Palette Rule.** These colors belong to Forward Deployed Engineering. Future books derive their own palette from subject and genre while preserving the Komal comprehension principles.

## Typography

The type system pairs a decisive, compact grotesk for orientation and headings with a highly legible humanist sans for sustained screen reading. A restrained mono face carries coordinates, artifact identifiers, code, and evidence labels. Exact families remain to be resolved through font testing during implementation.

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** Bold, compact, tightly spaced, and used for covers, part openers, and chapter-coordinate statements.
- **Headline:** Strong enough to establish hierarchy immediately without turning every page into a poster.
- **Title:** Clear sectional navigation for readers scanning a long chapter.
- **Body:** Comfortable at screen scale, open leading, and a target measure of roughly 55-68 characters.
- **Label:** Compact uppercase or tabular text for coordinates, evidence IDs, checkpoints, and folios.

**The Read Before Decoration Rule.** Body text never becomes small or tightly packed to make a layout appear sophisticated. Page count may increase to preserve legibility and rhythm.

## Layout

The book alternates three page modes: orientation, instruction, and decision. Orientation pages can use full fields of color and large spatial gestures. Instruction pages use a calm reading column with a secondary evidence rail or diagram zone. Decision pages compress the current state into a checkpoint, comparison, exercise, or handoff.

A visible route system connects the book: part, chapter, current coordinate, running-case milestone, and next handoff. The route may appear as a line, grid coordinate, tab, or field marker, but it always communicates position. Screens and PDF pages preserve consistent reading measure rather than maximizing words per page.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from layered map fields, overlapping evidence slips, inset checkpoint panels, and controlled tonal contrast. Shadows are structural and sparse; ordinary body containers remain flat. The book should feel assembled from field evidence without becoming scrapbook-like.

**The Evidence Sits Above the Field Rule.** An overlapping layer must correspond to evidence, annotation, or decision state. Empty visual layering is not allowed.

## Shapes

The form language combines straight map lines, clipped route corners, compact coordinate tabs, and lightly rounded evidence panels. Dramatic pages may use angled route movement, but body pages preserve stable vertical reading. Circles identify coordinates or checkpoints only when their meaning is explicit.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use color and layout changes to mark a real transition in the learning journey.
- **Do** design opening and closing matter with the same care as chapters.
- **Do** use figures, diagrams, tables, callouts, and exercises as explanatory page architecture.
- **Do** keep Komal Nakrani's authorship and edition identity consistent everywhere.
- **Do** preserve accessible contrast, searchable text, logical hierarchy, and descriptions.

### Don't:

- **Don't** copy content, illustrations, watermarks, brand devices, or distinctive layouts from reference books.
- **Don't** fill pages with generic stock photos, decorative gradients, or unexplained icons.
- **Don't** use the mascot as a default presenter; use it only when it materially improves teaching.
- **Don't** force the Forward Deployed Engineering palette onto future genres.
- **Don't** solve density by shrinking the type or weakening margins.
