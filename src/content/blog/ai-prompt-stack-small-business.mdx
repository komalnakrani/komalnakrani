## What Is an AI Prompt Stack and Why Does Your Business Need One?

A single prompt (*"Write a Facebook post about my product"*) yields generic, uninspiring results. 

An **AI Prompt Stack** is different. It is a structured sequence of 3-4 connected prompts where each step builds on the previous output:

```

│ THE 4-STEP AI PROMPT STACK │
```

## Prompt Stack 1: Long-Form Content to 10 Social Media Snippets

Stop creating social media posts from scratch. Instead, take one blog post or product description and run this exact prompt stack:

```text
Act as a senior content strategist for female solopreneurs. Here is my core article: [Insert Text/URL].

Extract the content and generate:
1. 3 Carousel Swipe Scripts (7 slides each) with high-curiosity slide headlines.
2. 3 Instagram Reel Scripts (15 seconds each) with visual hooks and caption copy.
3. 2 Educational LinkedIn Posts formatted with single-sentence line breaks.
4. 2 Pinterest Pin Titles and Descriptions with rich search keywords.
```

## Prompt Stack 2: The 15-Minute Weekly Newsletter Copywriter

Nurturing your email subscriber list is one of the highest-ROI activities in digital business. Use this prompt stack to draft weekly newsletters in minutes:

```text
Act as a warm, conversational email copywriter. My business is [Business Type, e.g., Notion Template & AI Coaching Shop]. My goal for this newsletter is to share 1 actionable tip about [Topic, e.g., organizing teacher lesson plans] and introduce our [Product Name].

Structure the email:
- Subject Line: 3 high-open-rate options (<50 chars).
- Hook: A relatable 2-sentence story about Sunday night overwhelm.
- Value Tip: 3 bullet points showing how to solve the problem.
- Soft CTA: Invite readers to check out [Product URL].
Tone should be encouraging, helpful, and non-pushy.
```

## Frequently Asked Questions About AI Automation for Small Business

### How do I stop ChatGPT from sounding robotic?
Always feed ChatGPT a 150-word sample of your own past writing before generating new copy, using this instruction: *"Analyze my voice, sentence length, and vocabulary in this text sample. Adopt this exact voice for all future outputs."*

### Can I run prompt stacks on free AI tools?
