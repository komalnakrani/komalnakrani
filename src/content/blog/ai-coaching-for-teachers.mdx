## 4 Custom AI Workflows Every Educator Should Master First

During coaching sessions, we focus on high-impact workflows that yield instant time savings:

### 1. Instant Differentiated Lesson Planning
Instead of spending two hours drafting three different versions of a lesson for varied learning abilities, we train ChatGPT to generate multi-tiered activities in 30 seconds.

> **Prompt Example**: 
> *"Act as an expert primary teacher. Here is my lesson topic: [Topic, e.g., Photosynthesis]. Create a 40-minute lesson outline with 3 activity stations: Station A (Visual & Tactile for supporting learners), Station B (Guided Reading for on-target learners), and Station C (Design Challenge for advanced learners)."*

### 2. Rapid Rubric & Assessment Generation
Generating detailed criteria rubrics for projects or essays used to take hours. With AI, you can generate a complete 4-tier rubric formatted in a clean table instantly:

```text
Act as a middle school teacher. Create a 4-level grading rubric (Exemplary, Proficient, Developing, Beginning) for a student oral presentation on renewable energy. Include criteria for Content Knowledge, Visual Aids, and Public Speaking. Format as a clean table.
```

### 3. Empathetic Parent Email Drafting
Communicating with parents about challenging behavioral or academic issues requires immense tact and energy. AI can draft a compassionate, professional first email draft in seconds:

```text
Act as a warm primary teacher. Draft an email to a parent about [Student Name], praising their recent progress in group work while gently raising a concern about missing math homework. Offer 2 collaborative steps we can try together. Keep the tone warm, respectful, and encouraging.
```

### 4. IEP Goal & Accommodation Synthesis
Synthesizing student data to draft clear, measurable IEP goals and classroom accommodations can be streamlined while keeping full teacher oversight and student privacy intact.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Teacher AI Coaching

### Will AI replace my role as a teacher?
Absolutely not. AI is a tool, not a teacher. AI cannot build a warm relationship with a child, comfort an upset student, or inspire a love for reading. What AI *can* do is handle the repetitive administrative paperwork so you have more human energy for your students.

### Is using AI in lesson planning ethical?
Yes! Using AI to assist with lesson planning, rubric creation, and administrative drafting is no different than using a textbook teacher guide, curriculum software, or online educational templates. You remain the expert editor who reviews and customizes every piece of content before it enters your classroom.

### What if my school uses strict data privacy rules?
During our 1-on-1 coaching sessions, we teach you strict data safety protocols: never input real student full names, private identification numbers, or confidential medical data into public AI models. We show you how to use anonymized placeholders safely.
