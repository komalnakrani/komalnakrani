### 1. The Multi-Channel Content Hub
Plan your entire content distribution strategy across Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest, and email newsletters. The Content Hub lets you view your calendar in Kanban boards, calendar grids, or platform-specific lists.

Each post card contains pre-set sections for:
- Hook headlines and slide outlines.
- Canva asset links and visual attachments.
- ChatGPT prompt helper blocks for caption polishing.
- Publication status (`Idea`, `Drafted`, `Canva Ready`, `Scheduled`, `Published`).

### 2. Digital Product Launch Pipeline
Whether you are launching a new [Canva Theme Kit](https://komalnakrani.com/shop/canva-instagram-themes) or an AI prompt workbook, the Launch Pipeline guides you through every step:
- **Phase 1: Validation & Research** (Competitor analysis and keyword mapping).
- **Phase 2: Product Creation** (Building templates in Notion or Canva).
- **Phase 3: Sales Page & Mockups** (Copywriting and visual asset design).
- **Phase 4: Storefront Upload** (Gumroad / Payhip link setup).

### 3. Client CRM & 1-on-1 Coaching Tracker
If you offer [1-on-1 AI Coaching](https://komalnakrani.com/coaching), the Client CRM keeps a private, organized record for every client. Track session dates, custom prompt stacks built during calls, session recordings, and action homework items.

### 4. Financial Tracker & Revenue Meters
Keep a real-time pulse on your business health. Input daily digital sales to see automated monthly revenue totals, expense breakdowns, and visual progress meters toward your financial goals.
