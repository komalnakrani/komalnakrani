# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Komal publications serve practicing and aspiring technical professionals who want to understand a role deeply enough to reason about real work, build evidence, and make bounded decisions. Readers primarily use the books on screens and may read for long sessions, revisit diagrams, follow running cases, and use companion exercises.

## Product Purpose

Komal Nakrani publishes original, role-based professional books that make complex technical practice understandable, memorable, and usable. Success means readers can move through substantial material without unnecessary cognitive strain, understand how ideas connect, and leave with durable mental models rather than isolated tips.

## Positioning

Each book joins original professional instruction, explicit evidence and authority boundaries, explanatory figures, a continuous fictional case, and deterministic companion artifacts. Komal teaches; the separate Abhyaas product independently certifies competence.

## Operating Context

Readers use the Astro website and downloadable PDF editions. The books include long-form chapters, part introductions, diagrams, tables, callouts, code or structured artifacts, learning packs, sources, edition records, and appendices. PDF editions must remain searchable, accessible, navigable, deterministic, and visually verified after generation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The first redesign prototype is *Forward Deployed Engineering: From Ambiguous Workflow to Production Outcome*.
- PDF design is optimized for screen reading, with larger type, shorter lines, generous spacing, strong navigation, and permission to increase page count.
- Each genre and book receives its own palette and visual motif rather than inheriting one fixed palette.
- Canonical publication figures are original PNG assets; Astro owns delivery optimization. Stored SVG and WebP publication figures are not used.
- Existing book content, sources, claims, figures, fictional-case boundaries, companion behavior, edition identity, errata, and role mappings remain authoritative.
- Reference books may inform craft and reading rhythm only. Their content, branding, illustrations, layouts, and distinctive graphic assets must not be copied.
- The existing light editorial Astro website is preserved unless a verified usability issue requires a focused change.

## Brand Commitments

- Every book is solely authored by Komal Nakrani.
- Author attribution must agree across covers, title and copyright pages, headers, metadata, edition records, and closing pages.
- Every publication is researched and written fresh for Komal.
- The reading experience should be explicit, polished, approachable, colorful when useful, easy to understand, and enjoyable across a long technical book.
- Visual elements must explain ideas rather than decorate them. Mascot use is optional and only justified when it materially aids teaching.

## Evidence on Hand

- Five completed published titles and their source-backed publication records.
- The current 338-page A4 *Forward Deployed Engineering* PDF and nineteen chapter manuscripts.
- Thirty-eight original Forward Deployed Engineering teaching figures and their accessibility metadata.
- Six user-provided visual-reference PDFs used only for design study.
- Deterministic PDF build, validation, rasterization, and site-check tooling in the repository.

## Product Principles

1. Comprehension outranks density.
2. Visual variety must strengthen the learning sequence, not fragment it.
3. Every page should communicate hierarchy before the reader starts reading sentences.
4. Original authorship, evidence limits, and professional authority boundaries remain visible.
5. A publication is complete only when content, PDF, rendered pages, web surfaces, metadata, and downloads agree.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain searchable text, logical heading order, human-readable navigation and references, descriptive figure captions and long descriptions, sufficient contrast, legible type sizes, and layouts that do not depend on color alone. Screen-first design must remain usable under zoom and across common PDF readers.
