# Appendix A - Deployment Dossier Templates

Each template states purpose, inputs, completion, and structure. Copy and version it; do not treat an empty template as evidence.

## OA-01 Role and discovery

- Purpose: establish role boundary, access, stakeholders, questions, evidence confidence.
- Inputs: role charter, authority, confidentiality, initial request.
- Complete when: discovery can proceed legitimately with named owners/gaps.
- Fields: objective; core/shared/supporting/out-of-scope; stakeholders; access/data purpose; evidence log; assumptions; escalation.

## OA-02 Workflow and problem

- Purpose: model actual actors, states, decisions, data, tools, handoffs, exceptions, failure cost.
- Inputs: observation/interviews/artifacts/operating evidence.
- Complete when: users validate the model and problem statement.
- Fields: journey/state model; decision table; exception taxonomy; evidence/confidence; incentives; root/contributing conditions.

## OA-03 Outcome contract

- Purpose: define outcome, guardrails, adoption, baseline, attribution, rights, non-goals, exit.
- Fields: metric specification; segment; source; cadence; owner; threshold authority; assumptions; decision triggers.

## OA-04 Scope and delivery

- Purpose: select least exposed representative path.
- Fields: must-prove/build/defer/reject; dependencies; uncertainty experiments; milestones; change control; stop/exit.

## OA-05 Design dossier

- Fields: structure/runtime/data/trust/failure/responsibility boundaries; interfaces; semantic data; environment; identity/secrets; AI decision/evals; threats/controls/evidence/authority.

## OA-06 Vertical slice

- Fields: runnable path; states/failures; repository map; config/flags; tests; doubles/non-proof; debt/owner/exit.

## OA-07 Verification

- Fields: risk/criterion; method/evidence type; data/provenance/version/segment; expected/observed; uncertainty/limitation; owner/disposition/waiver.

## OA-08 Operations, release, recovery

- Fields: promise/signals/objectives/alerts/runbooks; capacity/cost; artifact/config/schema/migration/cohort; stop/recovery; restore/break-glass; rehearsal.

## OA-09 Readiness

- Fields: criterion; met/gap/exception/reduced/block; facts/unknowns; options; cohort/window/gates; command/comms; authority/decision/triggers.

## OA-10 Stabilization and ownership

- Fields: command/impact/timeline/decisions/updates; containment/restoration/correction; verification/residual risk; adoption friction; task evidence; ten ownership areas; access revocation/acceptance.

## OA-11 Product and portfolio

- Fields: outcome review; pattern ledger; reuse proposal/defer/reject; customer isolation; portfolio record; delegation/memos; growth plan; hashes/state/next owners.
