# Appendix B - Gate and Review Checklists

Checklists summarize chapter models; they do not replace evidence or authority.

For every item record `met`, `gap`, `exception`, `reduced scope`, `block`, or `not applicable with reason`; link current evidence/version; name owner and decision authority; state consequence and next trigger. A checked box without executed evidence is a gap. A reviewer may recommend; only the designated authority can accept an exception or exposure decision.

## Discovery gate

- Legitimate purpose/access/data minimization and role boundary.
- Actual users/workflow/exceptions/handoffs validated.
- Evidence confidence, gaps, assumptions, escalation visible.

## Scope/design gate

- Outcome/guardrails/rights/non-goals/exit defined.
- Representative vertical scope/dependencies/uncertainty/stop defined.
- Architecture/interfaces/data/environment/identity/AI/control evidence and owners complete.

## Build/verification gate

- Clean synthetic path and consequential failures run.
- Contracts preserve semantics/intent/reconciliation.
- Risk maps to typed, segmented, versioned evidence and limitations.
- Security/accessibility/UAT/recovery gaps ownered.

## Operations/release gate

- Promises map to cohort/tail signals and actionable alerts/runbooks.
- Telemetry minimized; missing/stale evidence visible.
- Artifact/config/schema/migration/evidence/cohort bound.
- Recovery choice, restore, break-glass, and rehearsal executed.

## Production readiness gate

- Every criterion is met/gap/exception/reduced/block.
- Facts/unknowns/authority/options explicit.
- Cohort/window/absolute and comparative gates/command/comms/support complete.
- Stop/recovery triggers precommitted.

## Stabilization gate

- Command/impact/timeline/decisions/updates coherent.
- Containment/restoration and durable correction distinguished.
- Regression/monitoring/corrective ownership/residual risk complete.

## Ownership gate

- Representative users complete whole task.
- Customer demonstrates knowledge/access/telemetry/runbook/release/recovery/support/decisions/open risk/change.
- FDE privilege revoked or formally bounded; acceptance signed.

## Product/portfolio gate

- Invariant/variance/contexts/isolation/maintenance/disconfirmation explicit.
- One context does not become prevalence.
- Portfolio priority/delegation/memos/opportunity cost/review triggers evidence-based.

## Gate decision record

Use this compact closeout after any checklist:

- gate/artifact version and review date;
- exact scope/cohort/environment;
- attendees, evidence owners, and decision authority;
- met criteria and linked evidence;
- gaps, consequence, affected segments, and owner;
- exceptions with authority, conditions, monitoring, expiry, and recovery;
- supported options and tradeoffs;
- decision/recommendation/dissent;
- immediate actions and communications;
- widen/hold/stop/recover or revisit triggers;
- next review and source/build/issue reference.

### Fictional readiness example

Orchid's synthetic/local verification, bounded internal UAT, support schedule, and release record are current. Recovery access under the failed identity dependency is unexecuted. Disposition: `gap`, not `met`. Consequence: wider/safety-relevant cohort may lack an exercised recovery path. Recommendation: delay that cohort; a separately evidenced internal non-safety cohort may remain reduced scope. Release authority decides; security owns emergency-access conditions. Revisit only after executed current evidence is bound to the release record.

This example demonstrates checklist use. It is not a real launch or risk decision.
