# Chapter 17 Learning Pack - Make the Solution Usable and Ownable

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK

## Conceptual questions

1. Apply the ten-category adoption-friction taxonomy.
2. Why are use, outcome, support, satisfaction, and dropout complementary?
3. What whole-journey task evidence is required?
4. When is training appropriate versus masking design debt?
5. Distinguish accessibility criteria from usability evidence.
6. Explain the ten ownership-acceptance areas.
7. Why is demonstrated capability stronger than document/RACI delivery?
8. What access must be transferred/revoked?

## Scenario questions

- Technicians bypass suggestions with no visible evidence. Diagnose/repair.
- Usage is high because policy mandates it while support/dropout rises. Interpret.
- Customer has runbooks but cannot recover without FDE. Decide handoff.
- Customer can recover; FDE still retains admin. Decide transfer.
- One ownership area is missing at engagement end. Write exception/exit.

## Applied exercise and lab

1. Run `npm run test:companion`.
2. Diagnose five non-use signals with evidence/next test/owner.
3. Observe one whole-journey task.
4. Have a customer operator perform release/recovery/support tasks.
5. Verify customer permissions and revoke FDE privileges.

Pass only when capability is demonstrated and no hidden dependency remains.

3. FDE admin may remain indefinitely “just in case”: **false**.
