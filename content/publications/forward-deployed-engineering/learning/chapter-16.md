# Chapter 16 Learning Pack - Stabilize Under Real Conditions

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK

## Conceptual questions

1. Why establish command/impact before root-cause debugging?
2. Explain the five parallel incident tracks.
3. Distinguish confirmed, probable, and unknown timeline entries.
4. What belongs in a calibrated update?
5. Separate containment, restoration, durable correction, and stabilization.
6. What does blameless learning preserve?
7. What makes a corrective action systemic/verifiable?
8. What evidence permits stabilization exit?

## Scenario questions

- FDE is deepest expert but no commander is named. Establish roles.
- A probable cause and 30-minute ETA are requested publicly. Respond.
- Rollback restores service but stale config can recur. Define correction.
- Infrastructure is green while critical AI quality degrades. Diagnose.
- Service is restored but regression/monitoring/owner transfer are absent. Decide.

## Applied exercise and lab

1. Run `npm run test:companion`.
2. Build a timed confidence-tagged incident timeline.
3. Produce two calibrated updates as facts change.
4. Separate containment/restoration from three systemic corrective actions.
5. Apply stabilization exit criteria.

Pass only when command authority, evidence, communications, correction, verification, and residual risk remain coherent.
