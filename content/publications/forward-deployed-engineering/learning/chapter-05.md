## Scenario questions

### Three scope options

Compare a manual evidence workspace, bounded AI suggestion path, and automatic external action. For each, list outcome hypothesis, uncertainty, authority, failure consequence, recovery, and evidence yield. Select one under stated assumptions.

### Late identity dependency

The customer identity path will not be ready by the pilot date. Compare delay, reduced environment, scoped temporary mechanism, and architecture change. State who accepts consequence and what evidence remains invalid.

### Missing regulated access

A sensitive document workflow lacks legitimate data authority and representative users. Write the stop record and exact conditions for reopening without proposing production exposure.

### Voice request

An executive adds multilingual voice. Apply the change triggers and show which data, evaluation, accessibility, environment, support, and schedule decisions change.

## Applied exercise - `OA-04`

Produce:

- scope cone and selected vertical path;
- three complete scope options;
- must-prove/must-build/defer/reject list;
- dependency network;
- risk register with consequence/reversibility/owners;
- uncertainty-to-evidence-mode records;
- reversible milestones and evidence gates;
- range-based estimate assumptions;
- scope debt and change triggers;
- non-goals, stop conditions, and three-altitude defense.

Pass only when every included item traces to outcome/risk evidence, every external dependency has an owner/evidence path, and production is not used to discover an unacceptable control.

## Optional practice MCQs

### 1. Which path is smallest in the sense used by this chapter?

A. The fewest UI features.  
B. The least exposure that still crosses the critical uncertainty and required controls/operations.  
C. The fastest demo.  
D. The path with no external dependencies shown.

Answer: **B**.

### 2. A deferred user segment is included in the final outcome claim without evidence. What happened?

A. Efficient scope management.  
B. Scope debt leaked into an overbroad claim.  
C. The pilot became representative automatically.  
D. The baseline solved the gap.

Answer: **B**.

### 3. Which is a strong evidence gate?

A. “Integration starts in sprint three.”  
B. “Proceed when interface semantics, identity, error behavior, and owners are versioned and testable.”  
C. “Proceed when the demo looks good.”  
D. “Proceed when the deadline arrives.”

Answer: **B**.
