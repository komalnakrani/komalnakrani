## Scenario questions

- One customer adapter works well; leadership says platformize. Respond.
- Same interface appears twice but authority semantics differ. Classify.
- A severe safety gap appears once. Write a truthful product packet.
- Customer mappings leak into a shared module. Repair isolation.
- Shared module cost exceeds local duplication. Use disconfirmation.
