# Chapter 19 Learning Pack - Lead Beyond One Deployment

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK

## Conceptual questions

1. What belongs in a portfolio record?
2. Explain consequence/evidence/reversibility/latency attention.
3. Why avoid summed risk scores?
4. What belongs in a delegation contract?
5. How can executive/operational/technical messages share truth?
6. How does recurring escalation become system capacity?
7. Why must engineering depth remain?
8. What makes growth evidence-based?

## Scenario questions

- Loud customer versus critical irreversible migration: allocate attention.
- Delegate readiness review without decision authority: repair contract.
- Write executive and technical versions of one recovery escalation.
- Same escalation recurs across deployments. Convert it.
- A growth plan uses tenure/travel as success. Replace with evidence.

## Applied exercise and lab

1. Run `npm run test:companion`.
2. Prioritize four fictional engagements with narrative factors/opportunity cost.
3. Write one delegation contract and two-altitude escalation.
4. Build a six-month artifact/evidence growth plan.
5. Validate the dossier closure index.

Pass only when choices preserve uncertainty, authority, opportunity cost, and review triggers.
