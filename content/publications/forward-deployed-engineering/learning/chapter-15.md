# Chapter 15 Learning Pack - Cross the Production Threshold

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK

## Conceptual questions

1. Distinguish met, gap, exception, reduced scope, and block.
2. Separate facts, unknowns, judgments, and deadline preference.
3. Compare go, conditional go, reduced scope, delay, and stop.
4. When are internal, shadow, canary, cohort/region, and full patterns informative?
5. Why can a small cohort remain high consequence?
6. What belongs in an observation window?
7. What makes an exception formal?
8. What operational/communication preparation belongs in readiness?

## Scenario questions

- A control is documented but unexecuted at deadline. Classify and decide.
- Canary beats control but fails an absolute critical-quality criterion. Decide.
- A one-site safety cohort lacks recovery access. Explain why size is insufficient.
- Commercial pressure is high and UAT is missing. Build a recommendation.
- All technical gates pass but support/on-call access is absent. Classify readiness.

## Applied exercise and lab

1. Run `npm run test:companion`.
2. Build a readiness table with all five statuses.
3. Produce a go/no-go record with options, facts, unknowns, authority, conditions, triggers, communications, and recovery.
4. Defend a reduced-scope or delayed Orchid decision.

Pass only when missing evidence cannot become pass and FDE recommendation remains separate from owner authority.

## Optional practice MCQs

1. Deadline is launch evidence: **false**.
2. Small pilot means low consequence: **false**.
3. Exception equals full go: **false**.

## Advanced challenge

Run a conflicting-stakeholder readiness meeting with one material recovery gap. Produce the signed decision, cohort, window, absolute/comparative gates, command plan, and stop/recovery communications.
