## Terminology locked

- Outcome, leading, adoption, system/quality, and guardrail measures are not interchangeable.
- A baseline is a versioned measurement under a definition/boundary, not merely “before.”
- Recommend, approve/accept, execute, and inform are distinct decision actions.
- A production claim states its attribution level and limitations.
