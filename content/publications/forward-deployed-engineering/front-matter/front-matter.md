## Preface

Forward deployed engineering is difficult because the work crosses code, workflow, customer systems, users, operations, and authority. A convincing prototype can still fail if it solves the wrong problem, hides exceptions, mishandles identity, cannot recover, lacks qualified approval, or depends indefinitely on its builder.

This book treats a deployment as a decision system. Every chapter creates evidence that can reject a weak assumption and improve the next decision. The running case, Orchid Assist, begins as a request for an AI copilot and becomes a bounded workflow whose data, interfaces, controls, evaluation, release, recovery, adoption, ownership, and reuse claims remain explicit.
