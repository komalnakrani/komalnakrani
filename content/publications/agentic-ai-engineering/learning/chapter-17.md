# Chapter 17 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Bind each release stage to a named uncertainty, control, exposure ceiling, gate, and reachable stop owner.
- Distinguish replay, simulation, shadow, read-only, approval, and cohort evidence.
- Detect, contain, reconcile, repair, replay, update, and either re-release narrowly or retire.
- Separate rollback, autonomy reduction, containment, compensation, and takeover.

## Assessment

Complete a release table and retry-storm incident packet. Twenty points: hypothesis 4, effect containment 5, stop authority 4, correction evidence 5, limitations 2. Automatic failure if shadow performs effects or a patch closes the incident without replay.
