# Chapter 11 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Distinguish information request, review, confirmation, approval, intervention, takeover, and formal authorization.
- Bind an exact proposal, evidence, reviewer, authority, SLA, expiry, actions, and fallback into a durable checkpoint.
- Resume through fresh owner, authority, state, deployment, and budget validation.
- Handle rejection, no response, expiry, incompatibility, cancellation, and takeover without inventing consent.

## Assessment

Build a checkpoint type matrix and resolve seven FieldOps fault traces. Twenty points: type 4, authority 5, evidence 4, timing/revalidation 4, takeover 3. Automatic failure if execution precedes response, approval is generic, rejection retries, no response becomes consent, or chain-of-thought substitutes for evidence. Formative only.
