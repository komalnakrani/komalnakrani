# Chapter 02 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Build a fixed baseline before selecting autonomy.
- Compare fixed, assisted, single-agent, and multi-agent levels under held-constant conditions.
- Preserve the general multi-agent superiority claim as disputed.
- Issue a reversible `AR-01 v0.2.0` with rejected alternatives and falsifiers.

## Formative work

Score twelve synthetic FieldOps tasks on ambiguity, feedback, consequence, observability, horizon, containment, and cost. Select the least complex adequate level and state one measurable benefit and one falsifier per selection.

## Assessment

Twenty points: decision defensibility 6, controlled comparison 6, consequence treatment 4, disputed-claim accuracy 4. Presenting the injected 92 percent or a bounded research case as general real-world evidence is an automatic failure. This is formative, not certification.
