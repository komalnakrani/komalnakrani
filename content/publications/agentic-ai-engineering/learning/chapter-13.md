# Chapter 13 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Map bounded claims to direct outcome, action, state, effect, authority, recovery, efficiency, qualitative, and human-review evidence.
- Grade causal partial orders without punishing legitimate alternatives.
- Preserve consequence segments, trials, denominators, invalid records, grader disagreement, and uncertainty.
- Produce claim dispositions rather than a universal scalar or release authorization.

## Assessment

Grade six FieldOps traces and write the release-claim table. Twenty points: direct evidence 6, layered correctness 6, calibration 4, uncertainty/limits 4. Automatic failure for one universal score, model-grader override of authoritative state, hidden invalid-case removal, or synthetic production claim.
