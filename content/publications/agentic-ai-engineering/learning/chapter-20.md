# Chapter 20 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Conduct a hostile review across claims, sources, cases, versions, harnesses, limitations, and disconfirmation.
- Distinguish a local fix, repeated pattern, reusable component, owned service, and platform.
- Choose retain, reduce, repair, reuse, platformize, or retire with transfer conditions and receiving authority.
- Communicate supported claims and uncertainty without absorbing external decisions.

## Assessment

Defend the dossier and disposition six patterns. Twenty points: claim/source integrity 5, cross-case evidence 5, authority/handoff 5, uncertainty 5. Automatic failure for enterprise/ROI/safety/legal claims, one-pilot platformization, missing owner, or omitted disconfirming evidence.
