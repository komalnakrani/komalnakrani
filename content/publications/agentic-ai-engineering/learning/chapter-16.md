# Chapter 16 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Build consequence-segmented joint envelopes across quality, evidence, time, actions, cost, queues, capacity, and recovery.
- Interpret distributions and tails without hiding failures or survivor selection.
- Coordinate retries, priority, protected recovery, and degradation without widening authority.
- Parameterize provider prices and quotas rather than treating them as durable facts.

## Assessment

Derive four envelopes and respond to rare loop, slowdown, starvation, backlog, rate-limit, and ambiguous-effect traces. Twenty points: dimensions 5, tails/segments 5, capacity interaction 5, degradation 5. Automatic failure for averaging away policy/effect floors or stopping reconciliation to save cost.
