# Chapter 01 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Distinguish model, harness, product, platform, domain, and authority layers.
- Identify the component that selects each transition.
- Assign exactly one primary owner, evidence artifact, and escalation path per decision.
- Produce and review `AR-01 v0.1.0` without absorbing adjacent-role authority.

## Formative work

Classify the six companion decision records, then add six fictional change or failure decisions. Reject duplicate owners, missing evidence, unowned effects, and escalation without a safe waiting disposition.

## Assessment

Twenty points: layer accuracy 5, one-owner discipline 5, evidence and escalation 5, explicit exclusions 5. Pass requires no effect or authority decision without an owner. This learning check is formative and does not confer certification.
