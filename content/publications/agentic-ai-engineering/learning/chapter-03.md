# Chapter 03 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Define principal, beneficiary, goal, completion, non-goals, actions, and consequence.
- Bind approval to the exact effect and revalidate immediately before execution.
- Define budgets, stop conditions, escalation packets, and verification evidence.
- Reject proposal, principal, run, scope, time, replay, and prohibited-action mutations.

## Formative work

Build read-only, proposal-only, and one-effect variants of `AR-02`. Run all seven mutations in the companion fixture and explain why confirmation alone does not establish authority.

## Assessment

Twenty points: schema completeness 6, deterministic checks 6, authority accuracy 4, limitations and escalation 4. Pass requires every effect to have principal, scope, precondition, approval, verification, failure disposition, and test. This is formative, not certification.
