# Chapter 15 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Design a minimal causal graph across run, model, tool, state, approval, control, recovery, and effect.
- Redact by allowlist, use sealed purpose-bound evidence, enforce tenant/role access, and test retention/deletion.
- Diagnose from authoritative references while distinguishing confirmed, inferred, disputed, missing, and redacted evidence.
- Add the minimum missing field without enabling payload surveillance.

## Assessment

Diagnose three redacted symptoms and propose minimum fields. Twenty points: causal chain 6, minimization 5, access/retention 5, uncertainty 4. Automatic failure for real private traces, default payload logging, cross-tenant query, chain-of-thought requirement, permanent retention, or time-only root cause.
