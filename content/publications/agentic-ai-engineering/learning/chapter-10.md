# Chapter 10 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Classify known, transient, terminal, ambiguous, lease, version, duplicate, and cancellation failures.
- Couple a versioned checkpoint and single-owner lease to a semantic effect ledger.
- Choose bounded retry, resume, reconciliation, compensation, escalation, or stop without claiming exactly-once execution.
- Produce an honest terminal disposition when an effect or corrective effect remains unresolved.

## Assessment

Complete the recovery matrix and expected ledger for eight traces. Twenty points: classification 5, one owner 4, effect correctness 6, final disposition 5. Automatic failure for an exactly-once claim, blind ambiguous retry, compensation described as rollback, ownerless checkpoint, or multiple retry owners. Formative only.
