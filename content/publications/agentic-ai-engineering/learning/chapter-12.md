# Chapter 12 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Define a versioned synthetic world containing state, tasks, users, tools, policies, time, budgets, faults, effects, and consequences.
- Build 30 task cards with grouped train, development, and held-out splits.
- Validate solvability, ground truth, hidden constraints, shortcuts, lineage contamination, and grader alignment.
- State supported and unsupported transfer claims without equating synthetic results to production readiness.

## Assessment

Author the environment card and coverage audit for the 30-task design. Twenty points: state/task 5, policy/effects 5, faults/splits 5, limits 5. Automatic failure for production-equivalence language, leaked related tasks, silent broken-task repair, omitted partial effects, or a final-answer-only grader. Formative only.
