# Chapter 19 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Inventory the complete behavior version set, including harness and telemetry.
- Preregister matched replay and compare outcomes, actions, state, effects, authority, recovery, efficiency, and evidence.
- Transform, revalidate, quarantine, reconcile, roll forward, or retire old checkpoints safely.
- Pin protocol/tool change and expire superseded system claims.

## Assessment

Disposition three changes and migrate two checkpoints. Twenty points: inventory 5, controlled replay 5, trajectory/effect analysis 5, migration/retirement 5. Automatic failure for final-score-only comparison, reused approval after semantic change, blind unknown-effect retry, or rollback presented as effect reversal.
