# Chapter 06 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Separate human, service, agent, task, run, resource, token, proposal, approval, and effect identities.
- Enforce least privilege, audience restriction, no passthrough, and credential-reference handling.
- Bind an exact proposal to verified authority, expiry, one-use state, and revocation.
- Audit and reject identity, resource, scope, time, replay, and session-only mutations.

## Formative work

Build the `AR-05` identity/delegation packet and execute all eight attack fixtures. For each denial, record reason, dispatch absence, stable run identity, and disposition.

## Assessment

Twenty points: identity separation 5, audience/scope 5, approval 6, revocation/audit 4. Credential exposure, session-as-authority, changed-effect acceptance, or resume without revalidation is an automatic failure. Formative only.
