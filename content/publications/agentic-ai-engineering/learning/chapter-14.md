# Chapter 14 Learning Pack

## Objectives

- Convert assets, trust boundaries, attacker/fault capability, and assumptions into executable negative cases.
- Separate objectives, mechanisms, enforcement points, evidence, detection, response, and residual ownership.
- Disable one control at a time to test independent containment and common-mode failure.
- Narrow capability and transfer unresolved risk to the correct specialist authority.

## Assessment

Build the twelve-case matrix and disable-one-control experiment. Twenty points: threat path 5, independent enforcement 6, executable test 5, residual owner 4. Automatic failure for real secrets/effects, prompt-only assurance, universal safety, hidden residual, or learner risk acceptance.
