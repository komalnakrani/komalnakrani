# Learning Pack 12 - Run Experiments That Change Decisions

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK. These formative prompts are publication learning material only.

## Recall and explain

1. Why must dispositions exist before results?
2. Distinguish ablation, paired replay, shadow, online controlled experiment, and canary.
3. What does a plan hash prove?
4. Why is significance not release authority?
5. What is the difference between policy and quality gates?
6. When does evidence expire?

## Scenario decisions

### Preferred system loses

The challenger improves recall and fails a critical segment. Preserve negative evidence, choose disposition, and design the next discriminating experiment.

### Explanation preference

Users prefer generation, but certainty inflation increases. Decide using comprehension, fidelity, latency/cost, and guardrails.

### Unpinnable provider

Design version evidence, repetition, replay, and limitation when hosted behavior cannot be pinned.

## Applied exercise

Preregister one ablation and paired comparison. Freeze baseline/change, cases, versions, metrics, guardrails, evaluator, stopping, confounds, and authority. Produce reproducible plan/result hashes and a decision memo.

## Optional formative MCQs

1. Can a p-value authorize launch? No.
2. What follows a broken implementation check? Invalidate and rerun.
3. Should a failed release case be deleted? No; preserve negative evidence and follow adjudication/versioning.

## Advanced challenge

Design a non-inferiority provider comparison where cost improves but critical controls retain zero margin.

## Completion evidence

Pass when plan precedes result, hashes reproduce, paired/segment evidence stays visible, and the preferred mechanism can be rejected.
