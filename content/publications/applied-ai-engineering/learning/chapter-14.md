# Learning Pack 14 - Budget Latency, Capacity, and Cost

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK. These formative prompts are publication learning material only.

## Recall and explain

1. Why can every component meet its median while end-to-end tail fails?
2. Distinguish throughput, concurrency, queue, saturation, and sustainable capacity.
3. What semantic claim does a cache key make?
4. Why can batching improve throughput and worsen latency?
5. Which costs exist beyond tokens or inference?
6. Why is the fastest configuration not automatically Pareto-acceptable?

## Scenario decisions

### Hidden mobile tail

Aggregate mean passes while image-mobile p95 and quality fail. Define the decision report and disposition.

### Retry amplification

Calculate calls from demand, retryable failure rate, maximum attempts, and layered retry risk. Preserve the end-to-end deadline.

### Broad cache

The fast cache omits permission and freshness versions. Decide whether to revise, reduce scope, or reject.

## Applied exercise

Run three versioned configurations. Report distributions, cold/warm and critical segments, concurrency, synthetic cost, quality gates, semantic risks, and a bounded Pareto selection.

## Optional formative MCQs

1. Can p95 values be safely added across components? Not generally.
2. Is a provider price timeless architecture evidence? No.
3. Should timeout observations be removed from latency? No.

## Advanced challenge

Design deadline-aware batching that preserves permission isolation and show where sparse traffic reverses its benefit.

## Completion evidence

Pass when tails and segments remain visible, retries expose load, every optimization names risk, and selection preserves evidence and control.
