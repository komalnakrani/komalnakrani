# Learning Pack 21 - Lead Applied AI Decisions

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK. These formative prompts are publication learning material only.

## Recall and explain

1. Why should unlike systems not be flattened into one readiness score?
2. What belongs in a portfolio evidence standard?
3. Distinguish recommendation, decision, implementation, verification, and review.
4. What changes across communication altitudes, and what must remain fixed?
5. How does bounded delegation preserve authority?
6. What evidence demonstrates leadership growth better than title or model count?

## Scenario decisions

### Loud deadline versus high consequence

One migration has an imminent deadline; another system has unknown critical-segment behavior and missing authority. Order attention with reasons.

### Delegated tool pilot

Delegate recovery tests while effects remain disabled. Specify outcome, exclusions, evidence, stop, escalation, and formal gate.

### Specialist escalation

Prepare a decision-ready brief that requests one qualified judgment without dumping the full dossier or implying approval.

## Applied exercise

Create a three-system portfolio map with consequence, evidence gap, negative evidence, containment, recommendation, owner, authority, next gate, fallback, and expiry.

## Optional formative MCQs

1. Does implementation ownership imply release authority? No.
2. Should favorable evidence omit critical negative evidence? No.
3. Is portfolio attention order a universal risk score? No.

## Advanced challenge

Translate one evidence spine into implementation, system, product, portfolio, specialist, and formal-authority messages without changing facts.

## Completion evidence

Pass when the first stop decision is legible, negative evidence remains central, responsibility and authority are distinct, and others can execute bounded decisions safely.
