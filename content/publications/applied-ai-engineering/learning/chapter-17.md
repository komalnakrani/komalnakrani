# Learning Pack 17 - Release to Learn Safely

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK. These formative prompts are publication learning material only.

## Recall and explain

1. Which uncertainties require real-use exposure and which should not?
2. Distinguish offline, shadow, internal, canary, cohort, regional, and broad modes.
3. Why is readiness not a score?
4. Which dimensions define blast radius beyond percentage?
5. Why is a feature flag not a rollback guarantee?
6. What separates go, conditional go, reduce scope, delay, and stop?

## Scenario decisions

### Restricted segments

Text passes while image-heavy and ambiguous legacy evidence fails. Decide scope with and without authority.

### Critical signal

One permission bypass occurs during a positive cohort. Execute stop, rollback, evidence preservation, and communication.

### Delayed outcome

The useful outcome arrives after the acceptable exposure window. Choose a different study, smaller capability, or no release.

## Applied exercise

Create `PF-11` v0.1 with versions, population, exclusions, deterministic router, control, duration, signals, stop, rollback, communications, reviewers, and authority.

## Optional formative MCQs

1. Does a canary prove safety? No.
2. Can deadline pressure create authority? No.
3. Does stage success authorize automatic expansion? No.

## Advanced challenge

Inject partial flag propagation and control-path saturation during rollback, then defend the bounded recovery decision.

## Completion evidence

Pass when exposure answers one uncertainty, restrictions remain enforced, every critical signal stops, and rollback is rehearsed under current identity.
