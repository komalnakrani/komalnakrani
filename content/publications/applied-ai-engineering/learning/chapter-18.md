# Learning Pack 18 - Diagnose Real Use and Incidents

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK. These formative prompts are publication learning material only.

## Recall and explain

1. Why should diagnosis begin with symptom rather than model blame?
2. What makes a hypothesis discriminating?
3. Distinguish detection, declaration, containment, correction, recovery, verification, and learning.
4. Why can aggregate engagement and user consequence disagree?
5. How can incident traces remain useful without raw content?
6. Why is a code patch insufficient durable learning?

## Scenario decisions

### False lead

Model behavior appears wrong, but its version is unchanged. Design paired data/model tests and record disconfirmation.

### Seller gaming

Contain exposure, test data-context, product-metric, control, and adversarial hypotheses, then correct system conditions.

### Affected-user state

Metrics recover after rollback. Decide reconstruction, correction, communication, and authority without inventing impact counts.

## Applied exercise

Build a chronological redacted incident packet with one false lead, multiple contributing conditions, containment before optimization, artifact changes, recovery verification, and residuals.

## Optional formative MCQs

1. Does unchanged service uptime prove behavior health? No.
2. Does a rising proxy outweigh a confirmed critical error? No.
3. Does rollback close an incident? No.

## Advanced challenge

Add an unknown tool effect to the evidence bundle and reconcile it while investigation and recovery load are bounded.

## Completion evidence

Pass when model blame is tested, privacy is preserved, consequence is contained, and learning changes evaluation, control, runbook, and ownership.
