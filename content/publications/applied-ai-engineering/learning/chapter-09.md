# Learning Pack 09 - Turn Consequences Into Evaluation Cases

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK. These formative prompts are publication learning material only.

## Recall and explain

1. Distinguish scenario, evaluation case, and storage row.
2. Why must a case map to consequence and contract before a metric is chosen?
3. What five dimensions belong in the coverage matrix?
4. Why are challenge-set failures not prevalence estimates?
5. How can evaluator development contaminate release evidence?
6. What does a split hash prove and not prove?

## Scenario decisions

### Duplicate construction

A development case is paraphrased and placed in release evaluation. Identify grouping evidence, affected claims, clean-up, replacement, version, and disclosure.

### Ambiguous domain judgment

Two authorized sources disagree and no hierarchy resolves them. Define acceptable behavior variants, prohibited output, reviewer competence, disagreement handling, and authority without inventing ground truth.

### New generated explanation

The product adds optional generated prose. Expand the coverage matrix and explain why existing retrieval cases cannot establish explanation quality.

## Applied exercise

Create a versioned evaluation-set card and suite. Include ordinary, edge, rare severe, adversarial, interaction, and change cases. Map clause, segment, data state, mechanism, and consequence; assign construction groups; freeze partitions; compute a hash; inject and detect contamination; publish gaps.

## Optional formative MCQs

1. What does 100% on ten constructed cases establish?
   - A. Real-world quality
   - B. Correctness for every user
   - C. Outcomes for those cases under the recorded versions
   - D. Release approval
   - **Answer: C.**

2. A case used to tune a grader belongs where?
   - A. Clean release evaluation
   - B. Evaluator development
   - C. Production prevalence sample
   - D. It has no effect on independence
   - **Answer: B.**

3. What should happen after release-case leakage?
   - A. Relabel and preserve the score
   - B. Hide the exposure
   - C. Invalidate affected evidence, move exposed cases, create a fresh holdout, and version the suite
   - D. Add more duplicates
   - **Answer: C.**

## Advanced challenge

Design a paired migration suite for old/new providers where one permission violation blocks migration despite many wording improvements. Preserve configuration, evaluator, split, and version evidence.

## Completion evidence

Pass when every admitted case has a decision job, provenance, clause/path, segment, state, mechanism, consequence, grouping, expected criteria, limitation, and partition; missing coverage and contamination remain visible.
