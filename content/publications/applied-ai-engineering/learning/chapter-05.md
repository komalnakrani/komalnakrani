# Learning Pack 05 - Make Task Data Representative

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK. These formative prompts are publication learning material only.

## Recall and explain

1. Representative relative to which six objects?
2. Distinguish structural, semantic, and fitness validation.
3. Why is a dataset card a question system rather than proof?
4. Name five leakage routes beyond a target column in features.
5. What claims can seeded synthetic data support and prohibit?

## Scenario decisions

### Green schema

A dimension field is present and numeric, but sellers use it for three different measurements. Classify structural, semantic, and fitness status. Choose support, narrow, repair/replay, or stop.

### Duplicate release evidence

Two seller listings share a canonical item and near-identical features but have different IDs. They cross development and release evaluation. Explain why the result is contaminated and rebuild the split.

### Seller ground truth

A team labels purchases as compatibility and non-returns as successful fit. Create a provenance and causal critique, then specify what claim the signals may support.

## Applied exercise

Generate and freeze a dataset with a content hash. Produce a data card, segment register, three-layer validation report, duplicate clusters, split assignments, leakage map, and prohibited uses. Preserve a failed naive split and rebuild a grouped clean split. Trace one claim backward to sources and forward to its only valid decision.

## Optional formative MCQs

1. What does schema validity establish?
   - A. Representativeness
   - B. Permission
   - C. Conformance to declared structure
   - D. Semantic truth
   - **Answer: C.**

2. When a release case causes a code fix, what happens?
   - A. It remains untouched release evidence
   - B. It becomes development knowledge and release evidence must be replenished
   - C. Delete it
   - D. Average it away
   - **Answer: B.**

3. What does synthetic Patchwork data prove?
   - A. Real marketplace performance
   - B. Fairness
   - C. Deterministic software and injected-failure mechanics
   - D. Compatibility safety
   - **Answer: C.**

## Advanced challenge

Design a label study that preserves independent decisions, evidence, reviewer competence, uncertainty, disagreement, adjudication, and intended-use limits. Explain why high agreement can still be invalid.

## Completion evidence

Pass when every partition, source, permission assumption, label criterion, limitation, and refresh trigger is versioned, and data defects visibly narrow or stop the product claim.
