# Learning Pack 19 - Change Models Without Losing the Product

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK. These formative prompts are publication learning material only.

## Recall and explain

1. Why is interface compatibility insufficient for a model migration?
2. Distinguish unchanged, improved, regressed, unknown, and untestable.
3. Why must the behavior contract be frozen before paired replay?
4. What makes aggregate improvement compatible with a stop decision?
5. How do deterministic fallback and rollback differ?
6. What evidence proves retirement?

## Scenario decisions

### Forced retirement

A candidate preserves schema but changes abstention and tail cost. Build a change dossier and choose migrate, delay, reduce scope, fallback, or stop.

### Moving alias

The provider cannot pin a behavior version. Design replay, trace identity, expiry, and rollback evidence without inventing stability.

### Unavoidable deadline

The current path retires before equivalent behavior exists. Design an honest reduced-capability state and name required authority.

## Applied exercise

Build a paired compatibility matrix with at least one favorable result, one critical regression, one unknown, one untestable state, a deterministic fallback, a migration state machine, and retirement proof.

## Optional formative MCQs

2. Can a deadline change formal risk authority? No.
3. Is unknown equivalent to pass? No.
