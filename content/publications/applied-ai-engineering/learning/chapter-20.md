# Learning Pack 20 - Earn Reuse From Repeated Evidence

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK. These formative prompts are publication learning material only.

## Recall and explain

1. Distinguish mechanism, evidence mechanism, evidence form, local evidence, and product decision.
2. What evidence permits movement up the reuse ladder?
3. Why do thresholds, data, and authority usually remain local?
4. What makes two cases meaningfully independent?
5. How can reuse enlarge blast radius?
6. Why must a reuse proposal have a disconfirmation plan?

## Scenario decisions

### Shared runner

Two products use the same runner but different task cases, segments, and thresholds. Write the stable seam and consumer contract.

### Canonical ranker

One feature succeeds and a platform team proposes one shared ranker. Decide what evidence would reject or reopen the abstraction.

### Redaction utility

Two consumers share redaction mechanics but have different purposes and retention. Separate reusable implementation from local authority.

## Applied exercise

Classify ten assets into reuse configurably, copy/adapt, test again, keep local, or reject. Include owner, version, isolation, fallback, economics, and disconfirmation.

## Optional formative MCQs

1. Does duplicated code prove a stable shared seam? No.
2. Does a copied checklist transfer its answers? No.
3. Can a shared-service owner approve every consumer release? No.

## Advanced challenge

Design consumer exit and shared-service retirement without losing local evidence or product behavior.

## Completion evidence

Pass when the seam is narrow, independent cases are named, local obligations remain visible, negative evidence can stop promotion, and one tempting abstraction is explicitly rejected.
