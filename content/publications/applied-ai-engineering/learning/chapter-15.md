# Learning Pack 15 - Observe Behavior Without Betraying Users

> NOT FOR LIVE CERTIFICATION BANK. These formative prompts are publication learning material only.

## Recall and explain

1. Distinguish telemetry, observability, monitoring, dashboard, and diagnosis.
2. Why can a green service deliver failing product behavior?
3. What fields can replace raw prompt, output, and image content?
4. Why is drift an observation rather than a cause?
5. Why is feedback selected rather than representative by default?
6. What makes deletion an executable control?

## Scenario decisions

### Green service

Find a failing image-mobile segment using behavior, context, version, latency, and evidence state without opening raw images.

### Novel report

Route one critical feedback report through adjudication and evaluation-case creation without estimating prevalence.

### Deletion failure

Define reconciliation, alert, quarantine, proof, and authority when one trace store misses expiry.

## Applied exercise

Build a signal catalog. Each signal must name purpose, sensitivity, owner, decision, retention, threshold, and blind spot. Implement an allowlisted trace and prove forbidden fields are removed.

## Optional formative MCQs

1. Does technical trace standardization grant collection authority? No.
2. Does no alert prove no failure? No.
3. Is the companion's 30-day example universal? No.

## Advanced challenge

Design sampling and aggregation that preserve a low-volume critical segment while minimizing linkage and content collection.

## Completion evidence

Pass when diagnosis separates service and behavior, raw content defaults off, feedback remains bounded, and deletion produces auditable evidence.
