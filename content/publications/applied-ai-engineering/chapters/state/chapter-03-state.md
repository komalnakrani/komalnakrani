## Non-repeat instruction

Later chapters invoke the state model and authority map rather than reteaching them. They must map new mechanisms, evaluations, failures, controls, releases, and changes back to clause IDs. Schema conformance must never be described as semantic correctness or authorization.

## Locked boundary handed forward

Patchwork may propose evidence-backed candidates and bounded editable seller-question drafts. It may not create evidence, overrule known incompatibility, imply guaranteed fit, contact a seller, purchase, or enter excluded high-consequence categories.
