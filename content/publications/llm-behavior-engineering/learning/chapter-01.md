# Learning Pack 01 - The Model Is Not the Behavior

> Publication learning material only. Not an Abhyaas certification bank.

## Recall and explain

1. Distinguish response, model, inference configuration, deterministic system, observed system behavior, and authorized outcome.
2. Why is a screenshot a weak evidence record without its generating conditions?
3. Explain the difference between a working owner and a formal authority.
4. Why does open-weight access increase inspectability without making behavior self-explanatory?
5. What does the evidence ledger record that a confidence score hides?

## Scenario decisions

### Warranty approval

A stakeholder asks Mosaic Desk to add `warrantyApproved: true` because its explanations look reliable. Classify the proposed change by observation, inference, effect, required evidence, deterministic control, working owner, and authority. State the smallest safe current disposition.

### A model card says suitable

A candidate's model card lists summarization as an intended use. Explain what the document contributes, what it cannot verify for Mosaic Desk, and which local evidence remains necessary.

### Managed versus open-weight visibility

A managed endpoint exposes a model ID and request parameters. An open-weight candidate exposes checkpoint, tokenizer, and runtime. Build a two-column record of observable, configurable, unknown, and newly owned surfaces. Do not rank the candidates.

## Applied exercise

Take a fluent Mosaic response and produce a responsibility charter containing:

- the six system objects;
- four falsifiable claims;
- observation versus inference;
- evidence method and limitation;
- working owner and authority;
- unresolved-owner list;
- prohibited effects;
- recheck triggers.

At least two consequential decisions must remain outside the LLM Engineer's formal authority.

## Formative questions

1. A response cites `SB-4`. What is established?
   - A. The proposal is correct.
   - B. The source is authoritative.
   - C. The response contains that identifier under the recorded configuration.
   - D. Warranty approval is justified.
   - **Answer: C.**

2. Who decides whether warranty policy is correctly interpreted?
   - A. The model.
   - B. Whoever owns the prompt.
   - C. The assigned warranty/domain authority.
   - D. The provider.
   - **Answer: C.**

3. What should happen when a critical authority is unresolved?
   - A. Choose the most confident output.
   - B. Narrow or block the behavior and escalate the decision.
   - C. Add a disclaimer after the output.
   - D. Average several model responses.
   - **Answer: B.**

## Completion evidence

Pass when another reader can locate every behavior claim, generating condition, limitation, working owner, and authority, and can explain why the current dossier supports an experiment but no production or outcome claim.
