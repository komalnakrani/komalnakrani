# Learning Pack 04 - Select a Model and Access Posture

> Publication learning material only. Not an Abhyaas certification bank.

## Recall and explain

1. Why are model capability and access posture coupled but distinct decisions?
2. Distinguish a hard gate from a weighted preference.
3. Name responsibilities that move between managed and self-hosted paths.
4. Why do open weights not prove privacy, and why does managed access not prove safety?
5. What makes a migration seam semantic rather than merely syntactic?

## Scenario decisions

### Benchmark leader fails residence

A candidate with the strongest public result cannot meet the approved residence and allowed-data-interface gates. Record the disposition, evidence limitation, authority, and what would be required to reopen the path.

### Self-hosted without an operator

The team can pin weights, tokenizer, template, and runtime, but no platform owner accepts capacity, patching, recovery, or incident duties. Decide whether inspectability is sufficient and preserve the rejected alternative accurately.

### Managed deprecation notice

Use `LLME-CASE-013` only to rehearse qualification. List the exact current source to recheck, frozen evidence to replay, changed facts to record, and owners who decide whether migration may proceed.

## Applied exercise

Create a model/access decision record with:

- the `MD-01` contract and Chapter 3 mechanism risks;
- at least one managed and one open-weight candidate;
- hard gates and named authorities;
- common cases and evidence dimensions;
- observable, changeable, and unavailable surfaces;
- responsibility and dated cost/response-time inputs;
- one provisional selection and two justified rejections;
- a provider-neutral request/result/error seam;
- migration and requalification triggers.

Invalidate the first selection using one hard constraint, then identify the next reversible choice.

## Formative questions

1. Which fact can select a Mosaic candidate by itself?
   - A. Highest public benchmark rank.
   - B. Largest parameter count.
   - C. Downloadable weights.
   - D. None; the candidate must survive hard constraints and common task evidence.
   - **Answer: D.**

2. What does `not observable` mean in a managed-path record?
   - A. The value is unimportant.
   - B. The engineer may estimate it from another model.
   - C. The access path does not expose it, and the limitation remains explicit.
   - D. The provider guarantees it will never change.
   - **Answer: C.**

3. What does an official deprecation page establish?
   - A. All providers follow one schedule.
   - B. A replacement preserves behavior.
   - C. Managed dependencies can change on a provider schedule that must be rechecked.
   - D. Managed access should be rejected.
   - **Answer: C.**

## Completion evidence

Pass when another reviewer can replay the selection, see every hard gate and authority, understand responsibility redistribution, reconstruct each rejection, and begin baseline construction without depending on a provider-specific application contract.
