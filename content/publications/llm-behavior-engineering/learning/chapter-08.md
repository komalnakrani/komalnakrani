# Learning Pack 08 - Budget Context Deliberately

> Publication learning material only. Not an Abhyaas certification bank.

## Recall and explain

1. Write the usable-input budget after output reserve, template/control overhead, and safety margin.
2. Why do nominal capacity and reliable evidence use differ?
3. Distinguish mandatory, conditional, optional, and prohibited context.
4. Why is compression a versioned evidence transformation?
5. Separate oversized, stale, conflicting, unauthorized, and absent failure states.

## Scenario decisions

### Bulletin in the middle

The current stop condition remains in context but disappears from the proposal. Design a position test without claiming that attention explains the result.

### Other-tenant cache prefix

A repeated prefix would be cheaper to reuse but contains tenant-specific evidence. Identify required cache-key, permission, invalidation, and owner decisions.

### Mandatory overflow

Control, case scope, and required evidence exceed usable input. Choose degraded, abstain, or escalate behavior and make every omission visible.

## Applied exercise

Pack the synthetic ledger. Preserve output reserve, exclude unauthorized records, select mandatory evidence first, and inspect omissions. Then create absent, stale, conflict, and oversize variants. Design beginning/middle/end tests for a future real candidate and identify managed versus open-weight measurement differences.

## Formative questions

1. A record fits the advertised window. What follows?
   - A. The model will use it reliably.
   - B. It can enter the represented sequence; use still requires testing.
   - C. It becomes authoritative.
   - D. It will be cached.
   - **Answer: B.**

2. Which item should be excluded before model context?
   - A. Authorized optional history.
   - B. A current mandatory bulletin.
   - C. A highly relevant other-tenant bulletin.
   - D. Output reserve.
   - **Answer: C.**

3. What does a cache hit prove?
   - A. The answer is correct.
   - B. The request was cheaper under all future pricing.
   - C. The configured cache reused eligible input under observed conditions.
   - D. The evidence is current.
   - **Answer: C.**

## Completion evidence

Pass when required evidence is not silently removed, prohibited context never enters the sequence, all omissions and transformations are traceable, and workload/caching claims remain dated and configuration-bound.
