## Scenario decisions

### Authority-bound readiness

Behavior evidence is attached, but privacy, security, platform, domain, and release decisions are pending. Name the correct status and explain why technical completeness does not imply approval.

### Two simultaneous regressions

A provider replacement regresses Gujarati citations and tail duration while a revoked source enters retrieval separately. Contain and diagnose the two layers without blaming one model component.

### Adaptation pressure

A novel provider-replay failure appears once. Product value is undecided, no training data are approved, and adapter/template repairs remain. Complete the referral and defend `not-justified`.

## Formative questions

1. Which signal is the safest starting point?
   - A. Every raw prompt and response forever.
   - B. Version, terminal state, reason code, coarse segment, and duration bucket tied to a decision.
   - C. Direct customer identity plus generated text.
   - D. Unrestricted retrieval passages.
   - **Answer: B.**

2. English formatting improves while a protected Gujarati citation case and tail gate regress. What is the bounded migration disposition?
   - A. Release because the aggregate improved.
   - B. Hold the candidate, preserve the qualified fallback, and investigate.
   - C. Delete the Gujarati segment.
   - D. Fine-tune immediately.
   - **Answer: B.**

3. Who formally approves production progression?
   - A. The model.
   - B. The LLM engineer alone.
   - C. The designated release authority with product, domain, privacy, security, and platform decisions in their scopes.
   - D. The automated grader.
   - **Answer: C.**

## Completion evidence

Pass when the candidate is reproducible, signals are minimized and decision-bearing, stages and recovery are explicit, failures stay layer-specific, migration retains raw regressions, and adaptation remains rejected until all evidence conditions clear.
