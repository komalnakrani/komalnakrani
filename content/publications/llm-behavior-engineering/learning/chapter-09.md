# Learning Pack 09 - Design the Retrieval Question

> Publication learning material only. Not an Abhyaas certification bank.

## Recall and explain

1. When should a claim use parametric response, deterministic lookup, human search, retrieval-generation, or abstention?
2. Why must eligibility precede relevance?
3. What metadata makes an evidence unit reviewable?
4. Why are retrieval and generated-behavior claims independent?
5. Distinguish no evidence, stale only, unauthorized only, conflicting, and not retrieval-appropriate.

## Scenario decisions

### Similar but forbidden

The best semantic match belongs to another tenant. Show the sequence from authorization through candidate selection, context packing, proposal validation, and no-effect display.

### Exact compatibility fact

A governed parts system provides an exact model/part compatibility relation. Explain why deterministic lookup is stronger than fuzzy retrieval for the fact and where search might still assist a reviewer.

### Warranty approval

Better policy retrieval is available. Explain why this does not grant approval authority and design the bounded evidence packet for the authorized reviewer.

## Applied exercise

Turn "search manuals" into a contract containing the evidence need, selected path, source families/owners, permissions, freshness, evidence unit, query object, retrieval claims, generated-behavior claims, answerability states, trace, owners, and reindex triggers. Reject two unjustified RAG proposals.

## Formative questions

1. Which property makes a record eligible?
   - A. High vector similarity.
   - B. Fluent wording.
   - C. Permission, purpose, currency, applicable scope, accepted source family, and provenance.
   - D. A citation-like title.
   - **Answer: C.**

2. Retrieval finds the correct record, but generation ignores it. Which claim passed?
   - A. Generated behavior only.
   - B. Retrieval candidate evidence only.
   - C. Both.
   - D. Production quality.
   - **Answer: B.**

3. No eligible source supports a required claim. What should happen?
   - A. Use parametric memory silently.
   - B. Search another tenant.
   - C. Enter the contract's no-evidence abstain or escalation route.
   - D. Increase similarity threshold.
   - **Answer: C.**

## Completion evidence

Pass when the learner can justify or reject retrieval from evidence needs, preserve authority and provenance, exclude forbidden records before ranking, and hand Chapter 10 independent, falsifiable retrieval and generation questions.
