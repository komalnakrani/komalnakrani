## Scenario decisions

### Confounded improvement

A candidate changes checkpoint, corpus, and template and passes two new cases. Explain why it is rejected and design three isolated comparisons.

### Protected regression

The target mapping improves while Gujarati citation behavior regresses. Apply the preregistered gates without averaging the results together.

### Migration pressure

A stakeholder asks to use new shorthand failures to justify the held provider migration. Separate the migration and adaptation populations.

## Applied exercise

Produce the frozen `MD-09` baseline with inherited digests, exact system identity, paired replay rows, a layer-specific residual ledger, a falsifiable hypothesis, controls, authority handoffs, and stop rules.

## Formative questions

1. Half the apparent failures disappear after a language-code repair. What should happen? **Answer:** remove them from the adaptation residual and retain the repair as a control.
2. Who decides whether fixing the residual is valuable? **Answer:** the named product and domain authorities, informed by engineering evidence.
3. What does `eligible-for-method-selection-not-training` authorize? **Answer:** comparison and rejection of intervention classes only.
