## Scenario decisions

### Fashion-driven PEFT

All multilingual failures are proposed for PEFT. Use the MD-09 audit to split upstream and residual cases.

### Wrong objective

A typed mapping target is converted into preference pairs. Reject or defend the method using mechanism fit and predicted evidence.

### Managed/open-weight choice

Compare exposed identity, data handling, controls, operational burden, and rollback without declaring either path inherently superior.

## Applied exercise

Complete the intervention matrix for no change, repair, SFT, PEFT, preference optimization, distillation, continued pretraining, and replacement. Record fit, access, data, burden, reversibility, predicted evidence, rejection, protected criteria, and stop rules.

## Formative questions

1. Which action fits malformed language codes? **Answer:** upstream repair and regression tests.
2. Why reject distillation here? **Answer:** no qualified teacher or accepted compression objective exists.
3. What is the final MD-09 status? **Answer:** method selection complete, training not approved.

## Completion evidence

Pass when every failure class has a mechanism-matched action, plausible alternatives are rejected explicitly, the frozen baseline remains the fallback, and no run or adapter is created.
