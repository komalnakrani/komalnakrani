# Learning Pack 5 - Curate, Deduplicate, and Separate

> Publication learning material only. Not an Abhyaas certification bank.

## Recall and explain

1. Which identities must survive every transformation?
2. Why are quality, authority, and duplicate decisions separate?
3. How can templated signatures cause false duplicate removal?
4. Why must source families be assigned before rows are split?
5. What can and cannot be claimed from a zero-overlap local audit?

## Scenario decisions

### False merge

Two unrelated messages share a long signature. Repair the similarity representation without deleting the originals or merging task content.

### Paraphrased leak

A training candidate differs from an evaluation record by wording and whitespace but shares a parent family. Apply exact, family, and approximate evidence.

### Count mismatch

Stage input does not equal accepted, rejected, quarantined, pending, and authority-deleted totals. Decide whether the dataset can freeze.

## Applied exercise

Produce raw/normalized digests, transform logs, a rejection ledger, reviewed threshold evidence, group-based five-vault manifests, cross-vault overlap reports, reconciled counts, and a limitations-bearing dataset card.

## Formative questions

1. Does deduplication guarantee privacy? **Answer:** no.
2. Can one threshold serve every purpose without justification? **Answer:** no; thresholds and representations are purpose- and corpus-dependent.
3. Which process may read evaluation targets during authoring? **Answer:** none; only approved evaluation execution may access them.

## Completion evidence

Pass when all records and decisions reconcile, the false merge and real leak are distinguished, known families cross no vault, and residual uncertainty remains explicit.
