# Learning Pack 6 - Build Instruction and Demonstration Data

> Publication learning material only. Not an Abhyaas certification bank.

## Recall and explain

1. What separates a polished answer from an instruction learning record?
2. Which checks establish that a target is evidence-linked?
3. How do template rendering and loss masks affect the learning interface?
4. Why are negative, abstention, and escalation records necessary?
5. Which coverage denominators expose repeated-family concentration?

## Scenario decisions

### Unsupported target

A generated response cites a real source but invents a warranty duration absent from every span. Apply structural, referential, domain, and authority checks.

### Template mismatch

Training and serving use different role/control-token formats. State the disposition even if both render readable transcripts.

### Packing leak

Two examples are packed without a reliable end boundary and one target reaches the next prompt. Repair the configuration without editing token arrays manually.

## Applied exercise

Create positive, negative, abstention, and escalation records with train-family lineage, authorized evidence, claim/span links, author/reviewer provenance, exact template identity, assistant-only loss regions, mask statistics, and a visible coverage report.

## Formative questions

1. Does a schema-valid target prove support? **Answer:** no.
2. May the managed baseline's response become an automatic target? **Answer:** no; any generated candidate needs independent evidence and review.
3. What does the final status authorize? **Answer:** further MD-11 data design only; rendering readiness and training remain blocked.

## Completion evidence

Pass when accepted targets are supported, non-answer states are explicit, rendering/masks reproduce, holdouts remain inaccessible, and the training/serving mismatch blocks progression.
