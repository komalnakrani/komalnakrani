## Mandatory opening findings

The Task 00 and bootstrap contracts must preserve these findings until a Phase
09 audit closes or disproves them with exact evidence:

1. Reader prose contains factory and phase-control instructions, including
   blueprint activation language, production-specification directions, and
   private Phase 08 status text that does not belong in the finished book.
2. The three seven-chapter lanes use inconsistent H1 and metadata conventions:
   `Chapter N`, `MLE-CH-N`, and title-only forms must be reconciled without
   changing canonical chapter identity.
3. Machine-record originality classifications may hide repeated authorial
   frames, including an exact long repeat across Chapters 02-05 and another

   removing identifiers and inspected as prose.
4. Reserved visual paths conflict between the blueprint/register convention and
   Chapter 05, 16, 18, and Appendix G. Phase 09 must freeze one textual path
   contract without generating assets.
5. Ten living and two volatile sources require editorial-freeze rechecks. HTTP
   success is not correctness; access restriction is not automatically a dead
   source; current mechanisms require version/date/limitation/recheck evidence.
6. The accepted pre-close Phase 08 suite passed `263/263`, while two live-tree
   fixture assertions no longer match the closed lifecycle tree. Phase 09 must
   preserve the historical evidence, replay it at the bound checkpoint, and

## Whole-book QA contract

### Coverage and depth

- Reconstruct every chapter identity, word range, section, claim, source, case,
  architecture assignment, boundary, scenario, domain, port, lab, assessment,
  visual record, and dossier handoff from actual bytes.
- Verify that every required mechanism teaches decision, evidence, misleading
  evidence, failure response, authority, limitation, and next evidence rather
  than merely naming them.
- Retain exactly 7 parts, 21 chapters, 63 canonical claims, 46 sources, 160
  source-claim edges, 12 cases, 104 case-chapter edges, 198 claim-case edges,
  and 34 case-source uses.
- Retain architecture `22/17/10/12/8`, ports `5/105/35`, lifecycle
  `17/19/2/4`, chapter output `168/42/21/25/21`, and furniture `10/7/7/5`.
- Every canonical claim has exactly one visible primary teaching home.

### Continuity, terminology, and originality

- Normalize reader-facing chapter and metadata conventions while preserving
  title, slug, order, part, milestone, and dossier identity.
- Prove Chapter 07 to 08 and Chapter 14 to 15 seams and all adjacent chapter
  prerequisites, openings, closings, part exits, and forward/back references.
- Remove factory, blueprint, phase-control, producer, reviewer, private-package,
  and implementation-instruction prose from reader-facing surfaces unless it is
  genuinely teaching the reader and is rewritten as such.
- Run same-pack, cross-chapter, other-role, and internal twenty-word scans plus a
  near-duplicate review. Machine identifiers may be removed for classification,
  but they may not exempt surrounding authorial prose.
- Classify intentional reinforcement explicitly; repair accidental repetition.

### Evidence, truth, currentness, and authority

- Reconcile every material technical statement to a canonical source-backed
  claim or visibly bounded book synthesis.
- Keep reported facts, attributed outcomes, inference, forbidden inference,
  limitations, and transfer rules separate. Constructed and synthetic cases
  never acquire reported outcomes.
- Recheck living and volatile sources at editorial freeze; record retrieval
  result, document/version identity, changed meaning, limitation, and next
  trigger. Quarantine unresolved material rather than relabeling it current.
- Preserve the MLE technical disposition and route product, domain, evaluation,
  platform, SRE, security, safety, privacy, governance, legal, release,
  operations, and business authority to the named owner.

### Companion, furniture, and visual readiness

- Reproduce the 41-file companion, `BL-ENTRY` through `BL-20`, five equal ports,
  all 17 states, 19 legal transitions, 2 forbidden transitions, 4 reopen
  triggers, two BL-17 branches, rollback, deterministic bytes, and effect denial.
- Run all 33 companion tests and independent double materialization outside the
  repository; prove earlier dossier bytes remain immutable.
- Reject an explicit reopen that advances evidence from `UNORIENTED` to
  `CONTRACTED`; reopen must originate at a semantically valid later state and
  route to earlier evidence. Distinguish a declared transition table from a
  persisted executable full-chain scenario, and either execute each claimed
  branch or narrow the reader-facing claim.
- Prove that a second process cannot adopt an existing dossier, that ownership
  capability is process-local, and that deterministic equality applies to
  dossier records rather than random ownership-marker bytes.
- Reconcile manuscript lab claims and commands with executable behavior and
  retain the non-proof boundary for production, safety, performance, legal,
  privacy, business, and unknown retirement paths.
- Verify furniture routes and all 63 claim, 46 source, and 12 case identifiers.
- Keep all 25 visual records textual and exactly four raster candidates
  ungenerated. Freeze one path, alt, long-description, caption, dimensions, and
  provenance contract for later Phase 10; no image asset may exist.

## Parallelism and repair ownership

Tasks 02-05 write disjoint QA reports and may run in parallel. Their reviews are
also independent and disjoint. Auditors produce findings, evidence, and proposed
repairs only. Task 06 owns only the immutable finding freeze and may not edit
canonical files. Task 07 is the sole canonical repair/integration owner and must
apply accepted changes serially or prove disjoint byte ownership before parallel
repair. Task 08 reconstructs the resulting package without trusting Task 07
assertions.

Before any canonical repair, the four reports are merged into
`finding-register.json`, whose exact bytes receive a distinct Task 06 independent
review, commit, push, fetch, and clean three-way Git checkpoint before any
canonical edit. After that freeze, disjoint chapter lanes 01-07, 08-14, 15-21,
and furniture/appendices may repair in parallel when every path has one accepted
owner. Companion changes remain a separate lane and require a demonstrated
executable contradiction. Shared registers and verification remain Task 07-only.

## Lifecycle and closure

Phase 09 uses stages `bootstrap`, `audit`, `repair`, `integration`,
`pre-hostile`, `pre-close`, `final-content`, and `final`. Task 00 occurs while
Phase 09 is inactive. Activation creates expected child #86 with exactly
`phase:09-book-qa`, `role:machine-learning-engineer`, and
`status:in-progress`, then updates all four local authorities and live #79/#86
bodies. If #86 is unavailable, the plan must be repaired and re-reviewed.

The pre-close checkpoint contains the accepted QA package while #86 remains
open. Only then may #86 become closed `status:done`. Final state leaves #79 open,
no active child, Phase 10 as the sole next gate and inactive, and catalog
position 6 not started. A sole final verification binds the pre-close package,
final authorities, live issues, tests, Git equality, and stop boundary.

## Stop boundary

Phase 09 may prepare textual visual contracts but may not create or modify PNG,
SVG, WebP, cover, screenshot, PDF, public publication, web-reader, course,
certification, Abhyaas, volume 2, catalog-position-6, or next-role output. The
user's all-PDF direction review remains after this current book completes
Phases 10, 11, and 19 and before any new book or role begins.
