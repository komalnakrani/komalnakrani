## Objective

Research, write, illustrate, publish, and verify the complete Machine Learning
Engineer book or evidence-justified series as original Komal Nakrani work. The
reader experience must use the approved screen-first standard from the first
edition rather than producing an A4 book and redesigning it later.

may run in parallel when file ownership is disjoint. If role research supports
multiple volumes, Volume 1 must pass manuscript, visual, publication, and
screen-first QA before Volume 2 becomes the active book.

## Scope and volume decision

The number of books is an evidence decision made after role validation and
competency clustering. The decision is not preselected by the breadth of the
catalog role or by the size of earlier Komal series.

Prefer a single book if the profession forms one coherent arc from problem and
data definition through training, qualification, serving, monitoring, and
change. Split only if each proposed volume has an independently substantial
thesis, reader transformation, project arc, and non-overlapping operational
purpose. Each volume must be publishable and professionally useful on its own.

The approved creative system applies to every evidence-justified volume, but
each volume receives a clear identifier and may shift accent emphasis without
changing the semantic meaning of colors.

## Editorial direction

The visual and narrative system is **Learning Systems Test Bench**.

Its thesis is: every model is an instrument under qualification. The reader
repeatedly moves through:

1. setup;
2. fit;
3. stress;
4. qualify;
5. serve;
6. watch;
7. revise.

The metaphor must stay technical. It does not turn the manuscript into a toy
laboratory, generic science fiction, or decorative dashboard. Every bench
element must encode an actual model, data, measurement, serving, or lifecycle
decision.

## Palette and semantic color

- Bench Black `#121A22`: orientation fields and deep instrument windows.
- Instrument Blue `#2765D7`: baselines, measured evidence, and active lifecycle
  paths.
- Calibration Lime `#B8E34A`: a named threshold or qualification gate that has
  actually passed.
- Diagnostic Rose `#C94463`: drift, regression, invalidation, intervention, or
  rollback.
- Warm Bench Paper `#F5F1E8`: sustained reading pages.
- Graphite Ink `#1A2230`: primary text.
- Grid Steel `#D6DDE0`: inactive rails, plotting grids, separators, and table
  structure.

Color never carries meaning alone. Lime and rose require a visible label,
shape, icon, pattern, or text disposition. Neither may be used as general
decoration.

## Page system

The final publication uses 7 by 10 inch portrait pages optimized for screens.
The same accepted manuscript and figure records feed the Astro reader and PDF.

### Bench Zero

Opening matter includes the cover, title and authorship record, edition and
copyright record, reader prerequisites, the Machine Learning Engineer ownership
boundary, complete lifecycle, evidence legend, how to read a bench sheet,
routed contents, project/case orientation, and part map.

### Bench Setup

Part and chapter orientation pages show the task, data boundary, baseline,
model family, acceptance target, chapter claims, evidence state, companion
milestone, and lifecycle position.

### Bench Sheet

Sustained instruction pages pair calm long-form reading with the smallest
useful artifact: a split rail, experiment card, learning curve, calibration
strip, confusion tile, lineage tag, serving envelope, drift band, or comparison
record. Page furniture stays quiet enough for continuous reading.

### Qualification Gate

Decision pages state evidence available, population and segment, uncertainty,
threshold, consequence, owner, disposition, rollback target, and next evidence.
The page must distinguish a measured pass from an assumption or unresolved
condition.

### Closing dossier

The book ends with appendices justified by the final architecture, terminology,
sources, claims, figures, accessibility, edition records, About Komal Nakrani,
and a complete release-and-recovery dossier containing the selected model,
retained baseline, validation limits, monitoring triggers, rollback target,
owner, and next retraining condition.

Closing statement: **“Ship the model only when its evidence can travel with
it.”**

## Parallel execution and ownership

The active role uses four total execution slots without permitting shared-file
collisions:

1. **Coordinator/integrator:** sole writer for role state, root and phase issue
   records, architecture freeze, shared source/claim/case/figure registries,
   publication manifest, final PDF installation, Git, and GitHub lifecycle.
2. **Research lane:** owns explicitly assigned, disjoint chapter research packs
   and returns source/claim proposals for coordinator integration.
3. **Manuscript/companion lane:** owns an explicitly assigned chapter range and
   its learning, state, immediate-QA, fixture, helper, and test files; it never
   edits shared registries.
4. **Visual/independent-QA lane:** owns preallocated figure filenames and
   isolated verification reports; it never rewrites manuscripts or shared
   registries.

Safe parallel windows begin only after the inputs they consume are frozen.
Role validation precedes book scope. Research precedes blueprints. Blueprints
precede manuscripts. Figure contracts precede image generation. Publication
state changes remain coordinator-only. Parallel proof builds use distinct
output paths.

No second role or second book is active while this book remains incomplete.
When a lane finishes, it is reassigned to another disjoint task in the same
book rather than opening the next catalog role.

## Verification gates

1. The role verdict is evidence-backed `PROCEED`; otherwise production stops at
   the catalog decision.
2. Scope, chapter count, part count, appendices, projects, cases, and figures
   follow the frozen architecture rather than inherited counts.
3. Every public technical claim maps to an accepted claim and current source;
   every reverse source relationship is symmetrical.
4. Every chapter passes originality, continuity, depth, role-boundary, and
   immediate QA.
5. The companion is deterministic, local, provider-neutral, secret-free, and
   makes no fabricated execution or production claim.
6. Every figure is an accepted canonical PNG with exact registry, manuscript,
   provenance, dimension, hash, mirror, caption, alt, and label agreement.
7. The website validates, builds, and passes link/reference and responsive
   reading checks.
8. The PDF contains complete beginning, parts, chapters, figures, appendices,
   source/edition records, author page, and ending.
9. Every PDF page is 7 by 10 inches, searchable, nonblank, readable, and free of
   clipping, overlap, malformed tables, replacement glyphs, or missing figures.
10. Two independent builds must be byte-identical; a mismatch blocks release
    until its nondeterministic input is found and removed.
11. Every page is rasterized and inspected through complete contact sheets;
    cover, contents, transitions, dense pages, figures, tables, code, appendices,
    and closing pages receive higher-resolution review.
12. Public promotion occurs only after manuscript, source, visual, PDF, web,
    metadata, download, and hostile final QA all pass.
